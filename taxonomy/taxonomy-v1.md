# Rudolf Steiner — Comprehensive Thematic Knowledge Map

**Version:** 1.0 — breadth-first working taxonomy  
**Date:** 1 November 2026  
**Primary textual corpus:** Rudolf Steiner books, lectures and surviving artistic legacy

## Editorial method

Use this as an expandable taxonomic hypothesis, not as a claim that every lecture has already been indexed. The branches are researcher-created classifications of *Steiner’s own teachings*, not headings authored by Steiner. Keep a canonical topic ID, its primary location, alternative paths, and direct book/lecture evidence. GA volume references identify starting points for original-source validation, not proof of every individual bullet. Distinguish historical and spiritual assertions from established science, preserve translation variants and lecture dates, and critically contextualize discriminatory statements.

## Proposed atlas data model

Every terminal concept should ultimately have: stable_id, canonical_title, original_German_term, translation_synonyms, concise_description, definition_in_Steiner, primary_GA, original_text_url, chapter_or_lecture_title, lecture_date, excerpt_location, chronological_phase, source_type, confidence_of_match, contextual_notes, related_concepts, and editorial_review_status.

A topic may have exactly one primary taxonomic parent, with any number of cross-branch links. Different versions of a claim must be stored separately with their dates and source texts.
