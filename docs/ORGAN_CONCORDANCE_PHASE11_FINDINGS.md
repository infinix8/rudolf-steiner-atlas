# Organ Concordance — Phase 11: GA 313 III and GA 317 II, two complete English-paragraph reviews

**Research date:** 2026-11-02. **Primary texts:** [GA 313, 13 April 1921, Dornach](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA313/English/SOL/19210413.html), §§1–20, [German comparison](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA313/Compare_03.php); and [GA 317, 26 June 1924, Dornach](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA317/English/SOL/19240626.html), §§1–33, [German comparison](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA317/Compare_02.php).

**Evidence status:** every numbered **English** section in both lectures read, including historical/contextual passages. Individual claims extracted selectively; the entire German text has **not** been collated, and not every sentence has been atomized. This is historical research on Steiner's esoteric ideas, **not medical or developmental science**.

## 1. A structural connection: why the chest is not a self-contained cause

In GA 313 III §§2–3, Steiner characterizes chest/respiratory/circulatory organization as a **physical–etheric unity**, open to additional astral and I influences. He does not make the chest an independent generator of all its rhythmic processes: he says influences from the **head** and **metabolic system** meet in it, and that the external world enters through breathing. The chest is an effect or **meeting region**. This expands GA 313 I's primary-activity / imprint distinction and GA 314's threefold/fourfold model, but is a **present-organizational** relation, not a claim about the first creation of the anatomical heart on Old Saturn.

In §5 he links carbon/oxygen exchange to astral–etheric activity and qualifies the usual picture of sleep: astral activity supposedly continues from the rest of the organism even when the head-associated portion has withdrawn. Compare GA 313 II §26's **regional reorganization** during sleep; they are strongly related statements, not independent competing cosmologies.

In §§6–9 Steiner interprets falling asleep and waking as condensations of a complex of bodily conditions and classifies conscious hunger/thirst as astral experiences that can allegedly affect circulation when chronically disturbed. He also attributes delayed rhythmic illness to old grief or hurried thinking (§7). These are **historical causal assertions**, not validated diagnostic methods.

The outer-environment discussion in §§10–20 extends Steiner's cosmic/earthly polarity into sunlight, climate, magnetism and electricity. His suggestions that magnets can treat arrhythmia (§17), tuberculosis (§18), or other diseases do not establish efficacy and are not advice. **His explicit denial in §14 that infectious organisms are genuine pathogens is false** according to modern infectious-disease evidence. The original text is retained only as a documented historical claim.

## 2. Brain and living thought: GA 317 II's model

Steiner's reasoning in GA 317 II §§11–16 has four distinguishable steps:

1. **Synthesis vs analysis (§11).** He associates thinking with synthetic brain organization and willing with analytic differentiation in metabolic–limb organs.
2. **Receptacle analogy (§12).** He compares the brain to a milk jug: a vessel need not manufacture its contents. This is an explicitly *illustrative analogy*, not evidence that real neurons cannot generate cognition.
3. **Pre-existent thought in world ether (§13).** He claims that living thoughts already exist in the general **Weltenäther** and enter the individual etheric organization before earthly incarnation.
4. **Organ shaping and reflective consciousness (§§14–16).** These living thoughts are said to shape the brain/nervous system. Subjective everyday thoughts arise, he claims, as mirror images reflected from these organs. He also describes nerves as dying or discarded matter—**not valid neurophysiology**. The German source confirms terms including `Weltenäther`, `Milchtopf`, and the mirror-image vocabulary.

The lecture thereby contains **two different claims about the brain**: (a) the formation of a bodily organ by purported living thought, and (b) the organ's later role in reflecting thought. These must be separate predicate types (`formed_by_world_thought` versus `reflects_world_thought`), neither to be confused with a specific planetary-stage origin or a named spiritual hierarchy.

## 3. Liver and the alleged relation between intention and deed

At §9 Steiner reiterates the earlier GA 317 I claim that a subtle liver abnormality allegedly explains inability to turn an intention into an action. At §25 he qualifies this into a model of allegedly weak **etheric activity in the liver**, to be met by a claimed educational relation involving a teacher's astral body (§§21–24).

In §§27–31 he adds an especially problematic karmic example: a child's liver/stomach are described as resisting regular entrance by the astral and I organization, leading to alternating strong and weak organ feelings. He connects childhood disability and mental illness with unverified past-life experiences. These claims can stigmatize disabled people and families. The Atlas must attribute and contextualize them; **they are not legitimate explanations or diagnoses of anyone's disability, developmental difference or behavior**.

A notable structural point remains useful for understanding Steiner's own system: the same *liver* already has documented roles as alleged inner Jupiter (GA 128), I/warmth system (GA 314), inward nutritional sense (GA 316), future brain-force reservoir (GA 205), and will/intention intermediary (GA 317). These are **different relation types and timescales**, not evidence for a single exclusive planetary or spiritual 'owner'.
