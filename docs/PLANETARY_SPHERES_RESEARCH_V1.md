# Planetary spheres: primary-source concordance and editorial specification
**Rudolf Steiner Atlas · first research pass · 19 November 2026**

> **Status:** 8 structured dossiers, 12 selected source passages/works, source-level check completed in the linked English Archive views. This is a researched first edition, **not** an exhaustive German-language corpus audit. Claims remain claims of Steiner's anthroposophical cosmology, not confirmed astronomy, archaeology, biology or medicine. Source links go to the original Archive; this repository does not reproduce full texts.

## Purpose and required disambiguations

This manuscript is the canonical editorial companion to `research/planetary-spheres-v1.json`, which supplies the Atlas overview and eight individual planet routes. Each route displays three divisions: **cosmic activity**, **human development**, and **hierarchies/mysteries/time**. For Vulcan, the three divisions necessarily become **the hidden Atlantean Oracle**, **modern Vulcan beings**, and **future planetary Vulcan**.

The word *planet* changes meaning across Steiner's work. Always distinguish:
1. **Present planetary sphere:** an invisible spiritual realm or boundary, not simply the observed astronomical body (GA 110 I and VI).
2. **Great planetary embodiment:** Old Saturn, Old Sun, Old Moon, Earth, future Jupiter, future Venus, future Vulcan (GA 110 V). Old Saturn ≠ current Saturn; future Venus ≠ current Venus.
3. **Initiatory centre / planetary intelligence:** e.g., Atlantean Mars Oracle; later a planetary intelligence under an Archangel (GA 13 IV; GA 237 XI). Neither should be casually equated with the hierarchy filling a sphere.

**Textual cautions.** GA 110 VII explicitly notes a Mercury/Venus nomenclature exchange with modern astronomical terms. The seven standard present sphere boundaries are Moon–Mercury–Venus–Sun–Mars–Jupiter–Saturn; **Vulcan is not an eighth standard boundary**. The spiritual hierarchy map in GA 110 uses *boundaries of domains*, not one spiritual entity living inside the material planet. Multiple hierarchies may work within one domain; planetary Archangels (GA 237) are not substitutes for their domain's higher rank. The Rishis should not be assigned unsupported one-to-one planetary identities.

## Evidence key

- **P**: directly grounded in a checked Steiner passage (paraphrase; linked source).
- **S**: editorial synthesis of two or more passages, presented as interpretation.
- **U**: unresolved, contested or requiring German/variant collation. Do not treat as settled fact.

## Detailed findings

### Saturn
**P:** GA 110 VI maps the Saturn boundary to the Thrones. GA 228 I characterizes Saturn as cosmic remembrance and the past, in explicit contrast to Jupiter's living thought. GA 231 IV associates the post-mortem Saturn experience with remembrance among spiritual beings. GA 13 IV presents Old Saturn as warmth existence and the early physical human germ. **S:** The coherent theme is continuity or preservation, but it is **not** a statement that the physical planet causes memory via radiative force. **U:** Compare Steiner's differing treatment of cosmic chronology across GA 11, GA 13 and GA 110 before asserting precise hierarchy-by-substage tables.

### Jupiter
**P:** GA 110 VI assigns the Kyriotetes to the Jupiter boundary; GA 228 I calls Jupiter the thinker of the planetary system, disclosing living cosmic thoughts. GA 102 III connects Jupiter beings to the consciousness soul. GA 231 IV describes divine thought in the after-death Jupiter region. **S:** These passages show related but not identical functions: cosmic living wisdom, earthly soul development, and post-mortem experience. **U:** Further pass needed to compare the meaning of future Jupiter in GA 13 with present Jupiter sphere in every context.

### Mars
**P:** GA 110 VI associates Mars with the Dynamis; GA 102 III with the sentient or feeling soul; GA 231 IV with cosmic speech. GA 209 I includes Mars within the outer-planet consciousness polarity. **S:** 'Movement', 'soul feeling' and 'speech' should appear as different lecture-context views, rather than being collapsed into a single hard definition. **U:** Further primary work needed on Mars and iron across GA 102 and medical lectures.

### Sun
**P:** GA 110 VI associates the Sun boundary with the Exusiai and their guidance of planetary transitions. GA 209 I treats the Sun as mediator in the human organization. GA 13 IV describes the Sun/Christ initiation as exceptional; GA 109 contrasts the central Sun Oracle and hidden Vulcan stream. GA 237 XI names Michael as a solar intelligence's Archangel. **S:** The Sun's special place in the Rishi narrative concerns the highest solar mystery, **not** a claim that Christ is just one Exusiai. **U:** Full Christology requires broader GA 112/113/130 research and translation comparison.

### Venus
**P:** GA 110 VI associates Venus with Archai, directing longer human cultural development. GA 228 I characterizes Venus as receptive to earthly inward soul experience. GA 102 III says Venus beings participate as Spirit-Self enters the consciousness soul. **S:** 'Love' alone is too narrow and not sufficiently proven as the overarching definition. **U:** Nomenclature ambiguity with Mercury needs cross-work annotation.

### Mercury
**P:** GA 110 VI connects Mercury with Archangeloi, and GA 228 I discusses combinatory thinking. GA 102 III gives two activities: shaping the intellectual soul and instructing initiates. GA 237 XI associates Raphael with Mercury's planetary intelligence. **S:** Distinguish mental ability and the content of wisdom; Mercury is not merely a lesser Jupiter. **U:** Beware the old esoteric naming system in GA 110 VII; don't imply the modern physical Mercury occupies the same relative ordering in all lectures.

### Moon
**P:** GA 110 VI places the Angeloi's individual guidance up to the Moon boundary. GA 209 I puts Moon with the inward metabolic/limb polarity. GA 13 IV distinguishes Old Moon from the present Earth–Moon separation. GA 237 XI names Gabriel in the planetary-intelligence series. **S:** 'Incarnation and human destiny' summarizes a group of related passages but should not turn into testable genetic assertions. **U:** Jehovah's special role in lunar work needs a separate passage audit; do not assert all Moon activity belongs only to Angels.

### Vulcan
**P:** GA 13 IV §§92–94 says the Vulcan Oracle's leader had no physical planet of its own and acted in Earth's environment; initiates turned toward earthly phenomena, contributing early forms of science and art. Other initiation schools differed in degrees of ready-made revelation versus concepts. GA 109 (1909-05-31) says Vulcan prepared secretly beside the Sun Oracle. GA 204 XIV (1921-05-13) claims Vulcan beings are entering earthly existence. GA 110 V distinguishes *future Vulcan* as the seventh great planetary embodiment. **S:** A repeating Earthward orientation is a helpful motif, but no text reviewed proves the same personal beings directed all three contexts. **U:** No warranted one-to-one GA 110 rank or stable 'planetary intelligence Archangel' mapping for Vulcan. Do **not** equate Vulcan with a hypothetical intramercurial planet or UFO claims.

## Connections worth showing in the Atlas

1. **Ancient India → Atlantean oracles:** GA 110 I treats planets as spiritual worlds of the Rishis, rather than names of physical bodies. GA 13 IV describes the diverse Atlantean oracles. Resist the temptation to invent an individual Rishi–planet chart.
2. **Sun → Christ and the I:** The highest Sun mystery has a distinct narrative from the hierarchy that marks the Sun boundary. Keep a cross-link to future sourced study of GA 112/113.
3. **Human constitution:** GA 102 III maps Mars → sentient, Mercury → intellectual, Jupiter → consciousness soul; Venus → Spirit-Self's entry. These are *one lecture's* specific attributions, not a universally exhaustive single-factor anatomy.
4. **Human organism:** GA 209 I groups Saturn/Jupiter/Mars and Moon/Mercury/Venus into poles, with Sun as a middle. Don't turn these into medical advice.
5. **Post-mortem spiritual development:** GA 231 IV (Mars speech, Jupiter thought, Saturn memory); cross-read GA 228 I.
6. **Old Saturn → Old Sun → Old Moon → Earth → future Jupiter → future Venus → future Vulcan:** links to the separately maintained cosmic chronology. Never plot the seven present planets as though they were identical to these seven evolutionary conditions.

## Direct primary-source links and checked scope

- **GA110-I — GA 110, 1909-04-12.** [Spiritual hierarchies and planetary spheres](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/English/SOL/19090412a01.html). §§12–13: planet names as spiritual worlds, not merely visible bodies.
- **GA110-VI — GA 110, 1909-04-15.** [The planetary boundaries of the hierarchies](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/English/SOL/19090415p01.html). §§7–19: Moon/Angels through Saturn/Thrones; Earth-centered spiritual spheres, not physical astronomy.
- **GA110-VII — GA 110, 1909-04-16.** [Mercury and Venus nomenclature warning](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/English/SOL/19090416p01.html). Older esoteric and modern astronomical names for Mercury/Venus are interchanged in this account.
- **GA110-V — GA 110, 1909-04-14.** [Old Saturn through future Vulcan](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/English/SOL/19090414p01.html). The seven great planetary embodiments are not the seven present astronomical spheres.
- **GA102-III — GA 102, 1908-02-15.** [Mars, Mercury, Jupiter and Venus in soul development](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA102/English/SOL/19080215p01.html). §§23–25: Mars/sentient soul; Mercury/intellectual soul; Jupiter/consciousness soul; Venus/Spirit-Self; Mercury initiation teaching.
- **GA228-I — GA 228, 1923-07-27.** [The spiritual individualities of the planets](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA228/English/SOL/19230727p01.html). Saturn cosmic memory; Jupiter living thinking; characterizations of Mars, Venus, Mercury and Moon.
- **GA209-I — GA 209, 1921-11-24.** [Cosmic forces and the human organism](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA209/English/APC1948/19211124p01.html). Outer Mars/Jupiter/Saturn and consciousness pole; Venus/Mercury/Moon with metabolism and limbs; Sun as mediator.
- **GA231-IV — GA 231, 1923-11-17.** [Planetary spheres in life between death and rebirth](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA231/English/SOL/19231117p01.html). Human journey through spheres; Mars cosmic speech, Jupiter thought, Saturn memory.
- **GA237-XI — GA 237, 1924-08-08.** [Planetary intelligences and their Archangels](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA237/English/SOL/19240808p01.html). Michael, Gabriel, Raphael, Anael, Samael, Zachariel, Oriphiel; not identical with the primary planetary hierarchies.
- **GA013-IV — GA 13, 1910.** [Occult Science, IV: Cosmic and human evolution](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/AP1972/GA013_c04.html). §§91–94: Atlantis and Sun/Vulcan oracles; Vulcan Earth-oriented knowledge, art and science; planetary evolution.
- **GA109 — GA 109, 1909-05-31.** [From Buddha to Christ: the seven Atlantean oracles](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/19090531p01.html). Sun Oracle and the Vulcan Oracle preparing secretly; transmission of ancient wisdom.
- **GA204-XIV — GA 204, 1921-05-13.** [Vulcan beings and modern Earth](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA204/English/SOL/19210513p01.html). Steiner's claim that Vulcan beings are entering earthly existence; not an astronomical discovery.

## Editorial method and remaining audit work

- Each site dossier retains exact source IDs; source chips go to the passage, **not** to an unreferenced GA volume home.
- This pass checks selected English passages or work chapters. A future pass should capture German wording, exact paragraphs, translation variants, and complete corpus coverage.
- Uncertain links must be labelled as unresolved; editorial analogies or 'vibes' must never be upgraded to direct assertions.
- Archive text is summarized; do not mirror long copyrighted passages.
- Ensure every planet route has all three divisions, source anchors, and differentiation of present sphere versus planetary embodiment versus Oracle/intelligence. Vulcan must have three distinct special divisions.
- This is a thematic guide to Steiner's claims. Its historical, astronomical and physiological descriptions are not independently validated.
