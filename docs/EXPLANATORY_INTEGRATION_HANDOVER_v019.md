## 1. A two-direction developmental process
GA110 V §§5–8 (German and English parallel displayed) depicts a previous system maturing into agents capable of founding another; the First Hierarchy receives, elaborates and enacts a world-system aim. The German explicitly calls the language pictorial. This gives a direction from mature agents toward new formative conditions. GA107 17 June 1909 §§21–26 contrasts mere recurrence with creation of new content; Christ's deed is not compelled by prior karma. GA13 VI §1 (previously checked) connects new Earth achievements with future development. The conjunction supports a reciprocal developmental hypothesis: provision → capacity → appropriation → creative act → contribution → future conditions. Do not infer an actual continuous causal chain from these passages alone. GA110's future transition refers to a system; it does not identify every human individual with a specific First Hierarchy rank. GA107's account of Archai receiving human results is a different recipient and scale. The two should be modeled with separate propositions.

## 2. Agency: a difference in kind, not simply more power
GA4 X §§8,12 distinguishes the agent's own purpose from an externally prescribed intention; shared ideational content becomes individualized in willing. This is an authored argument, not a claim that the human being creates the universal idea world. GA4 XI §§3–4,10 rejects extrapolating human-style purpose to nature and history; the 1918 addition explicitly distinguishes a higher spiritual relation and collective result arising from particular human purposes. GA110 V §7 is deliberately figurative. The two are not automatically inconsistent, but there is no license to define cosmic aims as a complete script of personal deeds. The correct comparison distinguishes general developmental possibility, necessary conditions, ideal order, actual individual intention, particular act and subsequent consequence. Retain LT-X03 and LT-X11; LT-X18 adds GA4/GA110 scope conflict.

## 3. How formative activity may make freedom possible
GA239 25 May 1924, RSP1966 §§3–6: cooperation in prebirth formation is followed by a change in the mode of part of the resulting prototype. Steiner associates this change with the possibility of moral choice rather than a constitution compelled to goodness. This supplies a concrete esoteric model of formative agency enabling autonomy by not determining all activity. It does not mean destruction of the entire prototype, independence from inherited organization or proof of a physical process. GA26 19 Oct 1924 §24 (LT107) describes a transition from guidance acting within humanity to guidance that can be followed freely. Compare GA26 22 Feb 1925 LT162–164: transient image-consciousness makes freedom possible while the inherited/past self sustains continuity. These are complementary mechanisms in distinct contexts (prebirth formation, spiritual guidance, waking cognition). They are not identical with each other or Yetzirah.

## 4. A genuine source-critical complication
GA26 16 Nov 1924 §4 first says I-activity is conveyed by the astral body to physical/etheric organization, then says freedom-experience must not affect those bodies. §§5–6 and LT118–120 distinguish natural determination, withheld cosmic effects and spiritual relationship. Hypothesis: enactment through a vehicle differs from alteration of its formative organization by cosmic forces. But the sentence scope remains ambiguous, and a German critical/parallel witness is needed. Do not solve by silently replacing the wording. LT-X19. This limits any simplistic four-world chain that assumes every mental act becomes an external result.

## 5. Memory, imagination and reciprocity
GA26 22 Feb 1925 §§1–8,11–15,18–24 distinguishes temporary conscious pictures from a persistent background of memory and describes imagination becoming world content through Christ's activity. This is a more specific candidate for the return relation in SYN-98. Separate retention in the human constitution, re-presentation in consciousness, imaginative content and incorporation into the spiritual world. Do not equate Saturn/Chesed memory, lunar reflection and imagination. GA107 §§19–21 describes a different feedback relation: Archai receive the products of human intellectual and ethical life. Neither proves that every thought alters material reality, that every act is free, or that the spiritual world receives only positive content. No empirical claim.

## 6. B.O.T.A. public comparison and rival explanations
Official public The Open Door, introduction and Builders section, supports the practical importance of decision, effort, self-transformation and service. This is direct public institutional teaching about practice, not an assertion of metaphysically independent ultimate authorship. Divine Will branch v0.1 and Upper Tree/Vulcan private-witness reports retain the ultimate agency dispute; no private material or locators published here. Keep three rival models:
A: one ultimate author, individual decision as differentiated expression.
B: enabling spiritual ground, genuinely individual authorship (GA4 X strongly supports the individual moral act).
C: compatibility at distinct explanatory levels. To establish C requires a clear account of how the same act has two authorial descriptions without making the individual motive predetermined. Shared ideational content and noncoercive instruction support comparison, not proof of C. Foreknowledge is epistemic and causation explanatory; separating them does not prove either doctrine endorses open alternatives. GA107 German indexed wording Vorschau is a lead, not full German text verification; distinguish prospective understanding from exhaustive infallible prediction.

## 7. Worked tests
T1 (cosmic formation): GA110 differentiated aim/elaboration/enactment and GA239 collaborative formation: useful because both have multiple agents, but only the latter here explicitly links changing product mode to individual moral choice. No rank-to-supernal identity. T2 (speaking): prior GA102/219/231 relations concern conditions, not authorship of a particular utterance. A person considering whether to speak kindly or harshly illustrates distinct capacity, perceived situation, motive, chosen utterance, physical execution and effects. GA4 X supports the moral appropriation distinction; no claim about an invisible planetary cause of that utterance. T3 (memory and creativity): a musician learns existing techniques, remembers a theme, develops a new melody and shares it; the acquired capacity is not the new result. Compare GA107 relational novelty and GA26 memory/imagination, but aesthetic invention is not necessarily a moral deed or occult cosmic contribution. T4 (free cooperation): a student voluntarily helps a colleague after understanding their need, rather than because an authority orders it. Noncoercive guidance and personal motivation are compatible at the practical level; whether one universal Self is the ultimate author is not decided by the case. T5 (negative test): a compelling inner image or impulse does not count as moral intuition merely because it is experienced inwardly; the same outward deed may be compelled or free in GA4's argument. The Four Worlds and Tarot remain comparative coordinates, not a causal proof or diagnostic tool.
