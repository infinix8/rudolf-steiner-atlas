## Comparative findings

Entry 5 §§18–24 differentiates brain/Imagination, respiration/Inspiration and metabolism/Intuition. The brain is described as a functional reflection, not merely a visually similar shape. The claim that physiology alone does not settle materialism belongs to this argument. In §28 German says cognitive Inspiration where SOL twice prints inhalation. The passage therefore does not prescribe inhaling as a sufficient spiritual method.

Its §23 also explicitly requires material decomposition for will. This is linked as an unresolved apparent conflict to GA314 III §5's constructive volitional processes (ORG-CLAIM-V19-027). Different stages or scales could matter, but the present audit does not establish that explanation.

Entry 6 adds the route by which an allegedly gaseous astral radiation returns to form physical kidney organs. This differs from simply labeling the kidneys astral. Its oxygen, nitrogen and hydrogen correspondences overlap earlier v6 records; those earlier IDs remain linked. Its proposed renal-temperature experiments are research proposals, not observed results. The angel illustration is counterfactual digestive organization, not evidence of exclusive angelic builders. New fetal-membrane and gallbladder dossiers retain their limited source scope.

Entry 8 §9 gives nervous-sensory activity a primary formative role and describes soul activity as adapting to surplus formation. §§29–30 distinguishes a renal problem from an earlier metabolic blockage and relates renal activity to sweating. Unsupported diagnostic and toxic-remedy claims remain historical attributions.

Entry 9 §3 supplies a new sleep mechanism: I/astral effects allegedly continue by inertia after their agents are absent. German confirms this. It may qualify a simplistic absence-means-no-effects inference, but does not resolve every redistribution/interruption passage. Its therapeutic geometrical meditation is tied to an alleged post-treatment constitution, not general creative organ attainment.

The same lecture §§39–40 literally assigns bodily renewal to cosmic material received through respiration and senses, especially ears, while restricting food's material role to nervous organization. German confirms the claim; it is scientifically false nutrition theory, not a mistranslated metaphor. The new nail dossier records the explicit regrowth example without endorsing it.
