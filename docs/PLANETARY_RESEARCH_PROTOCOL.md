# Planetary research protocol — Venus coverage, Sun exposition

**Version:** 1.0 · 26 November 2026  
**Scope:** Mercury, Jupiter, Saturn, Vulcan, and future revisions of all planetary studies.  
**Purpose:** A reusable instruction document for ChatGPT Chat and Work sessions, producing both an auditable research corpus and a coherent explanatory book.

## 1. Governing principle

**Use the Venus research methodology and the Sun writing methodology.**

The Venus study established stable topic identifiers (V001–V288), multiple breadth searches, a source ledger, an explanatory coverage tracker and cumulative versioned manuscripts. Its v2.0 reader addressed 48 clusters and 288 topics. The Sun explanatory reader organized 115 subjects into nine larger thematic parts, prioritizing sustained explanations, conceptual connections and readable prose. These are precedents, not required numerical targets. Their topic granularity and available sources differ; the comparison is not a controlled test of Chat versus Work.

The desired product is a book that can be studied from beginning to end, with the completeness and traceability of a research database. Do not confuse a source citation, a topic heading, an evidence classification or a long PDF with substantive explanatory coverage.

Follow [SOURCE_METHOD.md](SOURCE_METHOD.md), [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md), [CONTENT_MODEL.md](CONTENT_MODEL.md) and [ATLAS_PUBLICATION_GUIDE.md](ATLAS_PUBLICATION_GUIDE.md). This protocol supplements those documents.

## 2. Non-negotiable rules

- Preserve earlier research, stable IDs, source records, unresolved questions and original versions. Never silently overwrite or renumber them. Reuse existing Jupiter research rather than start over.
- Use Steiner's authored works, dated lectures, directly attributed notes and artistic instructions as primary evidence. Distinguish books from lecture transcripts, notebooks, esoteric-school recollections and editorial additions. A GA number alone is not proof.
- Use exact, durable source URLs, chapter or lecture date, page/paragraph locator, edition/translation, date checked and a precise paraphrase. Check German originals when terminology or editions conflict. Record failed searches and inaccessible sources honestly.
- Distinguish explicit Steiner teaching, cross-source synthesis, explanatory analogy, external comparison, hypothesis and unresolved attribution. A verified passage proves what the text says, not that its spiritual, historical or medical claims are empirically true.
- Keep the present physical planet, spiritual sphere, planetary individuality, associated beings, historical mystery stream, symbolic correspondence and past/future world-embodiment separate. Assign context-specific relationships rather than exclusive planetary ownership.
- Treat BOTA/Hermetic correspondences as a separate comparative framework. Use public or authorized private material only; do not publish private course texts or reproduce copyrighted material beyond permitted excerpts. Do not import claims from later anthroposophists into Steiner's evidence ledger as if he said them.
- Never invent detailed mechanisms, hierarchical assignments, evolutionary subdivisions or quotations where the material does not support them. Explain the limit instead.
- Do substantial work in each iteration; avoid routine confirmation requests, repeated broad searches and premature declarations of completion. Save concrete outputs and a resume point after each pass. No unattended work is implied.

## 3. Phase A — Multi-round breadth research (prefer Chat)

### A0. Resume and orient

Read the latest research files, previous chat handover, existing Atlas planetary pages, and relevant earlier Sun/Moon/Mars/Venus studies. Identify already-known topics and open issues; record input versions and actual files accessible. Compare against existing organ, chronology and hierarchy research. Do not claim to have read unavailable material.

### A1. Systematic discovery rounds

Search the archive in English and German, across dates and GA cycles, using synonyms and related beings, processes, organs, symbols, and historical contexts. Perform several complementary rounds:

1. Explicit planetary references, meanings, spiritual beings and hierarchy domains.
2. Planetary interaction, cosmic origins, evolution, mystery schools, cultural epochs and future conditions.
3. Karma, life between death and rebirth, incarnation, biography, freedom, the I, soul and spiritual cognition.
4. Fourfold/threefold/ninefold human constitution, organs, physiological processes, metals, medicine and natural kingdoms.
5. Art, speech, music, eurythmy, architecture, agriculture, zodiac, symbolic and esoteric practices.
6. German terminology, variant editions, apparent contradictions, indirect connections, comparative BOTA themes and neglected/negative results.

Each round should add genuinely new substantive subjects, sharpen existing questions or improve evidence. An indirect association is a lead, not a direct correspondence. Map overlaps and use crosslinks instead of inflating counts.

### A2. Breadth saturation gate

Move to depth when two successive targeted passes mainly reinforce existing material or resolve evidence gaps, rather than reveal new major domains. This is a judgment, not a guarantee of exhaustive coverage of the entire GA. Record searches, areas not checked and rationale for stopping. Do not impose a target of 288 topics or 48 clusters on every planet.

### A3. Canonical handover

Produce the master Markdown, complete topic inventory and evidence matrix before transferring to Work. Give each topic one stable ID, canonical question, thematic cluster, proposed chapter, source pointers and status. Include rejected claims and gaps. Preserve IDs even if the later narrative combines several topics.

## 4. Phase B — Explanatory depth (prefer Work)

Read the handover and verify the most important sources directly. Construct a provisional **9–15 thematic part** structure adapted to the planet, rather than converting the original breadth clusters into hundreds of short disconnected entries. A chapter may address many topic IDs together. Map every ID to its section(s) before writing.

### Suggested book architecture (adapt, do not force)

1. Reader orientation, terminology and the planetary sphere.
2. Cosmic structure, spiritual beings and hierarchies.
3. Planetary interactions and distinctive spiritual activity.
4. Cosmic evolution, past conditions and the origin of the sphere.
5. Mystery schools, cultural history and spiritual streams.
6. Karma, death, reincarnation and destiny.
7. The I, soul, consciousness, freedom and initiation.
8. The human organism, development, organs and substances.
9. Natural kingdoms, earthly processes and rhythms.
10. Art, language, music, symbolism and esoteric practice.
11. Future planetary evolution and higher human capacities.
12. Comparative Tree of Life/BOTA, cross-planet synthesis and open questions.

Use a smaller or larger number of coherent parts when the evidence warrants it. Present macrocosm to microcosm with a clear progression; connect future evolution and practice at the end or where they naturally belong. Include a chapter introduction, connected exposition, meaningful transitions, crosslinks and concluding synthesis. Preserve the inventory as a concordance, not the book's narrative skeleton.

### Sun-style treatment of each substantive teaching

- Establish what Steiner means in the particular passage and why he introduces it.
- Explain the process in plain language, step by step when the text supports a sequence.
- Describe the beings/forces involved, their activity, what receives or transforms that activity and what changes; explicitly mark missing mechanisms.
- Relate it to human experience, constitution, consciousness, biography or cosmic development where warranted.
- Compare relevant lectures, explain differences in context or terminology, and separate textual assertions from interpretation.
- Use concrete examples or analogies where they genuinely help; identify them as editorial illustrations, not additional evidence.
- Show how the concept relates to other planets without manufacturing symmetries or making the current planet responsible for everything.
- Integrate dated GA references into the explanatory text, using exact locators and evidence IDs. Give the reader a route to the original passage.
- End difficult sections with a useful synthesis of what is known and what remains open.

Favor sustained explanatory prose over repetitive template headings, bullet catalogues or word-count padding. A short but precise treatment is appropriate when sources are limited. Core teachings deserve several connected paragraphs or pages where evidence supports that depth.

### B1–B3. Execution

B1: Draft the central cosmology and narrative architecture; verify high-impact sources and contradictions first.  
B2: Expand all thematic parts, including obscure leads, and update topic-to-chapter mapping. Work in substantial batches, maintaining the cumulative manuscript.  
B3: Revisit thin or fragmented sections, connect parallel topics, improve examples, verify citations and add purposeful diagrams. Avoid a separate second explanatory phase caused by producing a premature catalogue PDF.

A topic is not complete merely because a paragraph exists. Use the adequacy gate below before producing the final reader edition.

## 5. Phase C — Independent coverage and source audit

Maintain two independent dimensions:

| Dimension | Allowed statuses / meaning |
|---|---|
| Source review | lead, located, verified, contested, unavailable (consistent with SOURCE_METHOD.md; unavailable is a documented blocker) |
| Claim type | direct, synthesis, analogy, comparative, hypothesis, unresolved, rejected |
| Explanatory review | pending, drafted, needs-depth, needs-source, adequate, bounded, not-supported |
| Final disposition | included with explanation, included as qualified boundary, merged with canonical explanation, rejected with reason |

For each ID, inspect the actual explanation (not just presence of a heading). Ask:
- Does it answer the original question? Is the relevant context explained?
- Are the spiritual activity, relationships and significance clear, or is the text merely a correspondence?
- Are claims supported by exact sources? Are source conflicts and interpretive limits visible?
- If merged with other IDs, does the mapped section actually cover each original question?
- Is the treatment adequate for the available evidence? If not, mark a specific next action and repair it.

Use a separate audit pass over all IDs, ideally with a fresh reviewer/context. Recheck high-impact claims and all disputed/weak links. Count statuses separately, and report how many primary passages were actually reopened. Do not conflate structural coverage with independent source verification or explanatory adequacy. Record audit date and reviewer/method. Do not give an unsupported numerical score for quality.

**Completion gate:** every original ID has an adequate explanatory location or an explicit, justified bounded/not-supported disposition; none are silently dropped; no material outstanding needs-depth item is presented as finished. All important open source questions remain visible. The narrative is coherent, readable and source-linked. Exhaustive verification of the complete GA is not claimed unless actually performed.

## 6. Phase D — Reader PDF and reproducibility

Generate the PDF from the reviewed explanatory manuscript. Prefer a readable book-like layout (approximately 11–12 pt body, comfortable leading, meaningful headings, adequate margins, restrained color). Avoid shrinking text to meet a page count. Include a hierarchical linked contents, PDF bookmarks, page numbers, useful figures and captions, glossary, source register and topic-to-page concordance. Provide a clear statement that esoteric and medical claims are presented historically, not as established science or treatment recommendations.

Diagrams should clarify relationships and mechanisms, not merely decorate pages; label editorial models and contextual boundaries. Do not reuse conceptual diagrams as evidence.

Render and inspect all pages at least in contact sheets, check representative pages at full size, check broken internal links/bookmarks, table clipping, orphan headings, font legibility and source links. Report what was actually checked. Verify topic IDs and source references against the ledger and actual page mapping. Keep final editable manuscript and prior versions.

## 7. Canonical files and data contracts

Suggested location: `docs/research/planetary/<planet>/` (adapt to existing repository conventions; do not move earlier material without a migration map).

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| <PLANET>_MASTER_RESEARCH.md | Scope, version, searches, discoveries, source issues, handover, next steps |
| <PLANET>_TOPIC_INVENTORY.csv | Stable IDs, original questions, canonical clusters, aliases, related IDs |
| <PLANET>_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.csv | Topic-to-evidence mapping, contextual classification and exact locators |
| <PLANET>_SOURCE_REGISTER.md or .csv | Evidence IDs, full bibliographic metadata, source URLs, precise summaries, edition notes |
| <PLANET>_EXPLANATORY_MANUSCRIPT.md | Coherent Sun-style narrative, inline references and anchors |
| <PLANET>_EXPLANATORY_COVERAGE.csv | One row per original topic; actual section/page mapping, adequacy judgment and next action |
| <Planet>_Explanatory_Reader_vX.Y.pdf | Reviewed reader edition |
| CHANGELOG.md | Changes, decisions, versions and unresolved limitations |

Topic ID prefixes: MER, JUP, SAT, VUL (e.g. MER001), unless an existing inventory already uses a different stable scheme. Evidence IDs are separately namespaced, e.g. MER-E001. Reuse existing IDs where possible; record aliases and links across planets.

Evidence matrix minimum fields: topic_id, evidence_id, ga, work_title, source_type, lecture_date_or_chapter, locator, edition_translation, exact_url, checked_date, planetary_context, claim_type, source_status, supported_claim, caveat, related_ids. Use one row per topic-evidence relationship or a normalized separate join table.

Coverage matrix minimum fields: topic_id, original_question, cluster, part, section_anchor, pdf_page, evidence_ids, explanatory_status, final_disposition, reviewer_note, next_action, audit_date. Multiple IDs may map to the same substantive explanation. Word counts may flag thin sections but never prove adequacy.

After each pass, report: actual files and version, topics discovered or covered, review status counts, sources newly checked, key corrections, gaps, decisions, next steps. Store snapshots or Git commits; maintain one clearly identified current version. Avoid publication churn.

## 9. Phase E — Atlas integration and release

Only integrate reviewed claims. Preserve existing site pages, chronology dials, organ atlas, research nodes and POCs. Use canonical IDs and source URLs; map relationships to the existing knowledge graph and taxonomy. Link readers to the research PDF and source index when available. Record explicit contextual distinctions in data and UI. Run the repository validation checks and review diffs before merging substantive website/data changes.

Keep documentation-only work separate from website publication. Follow selective publishing rules in ATLAS_PUBLICATION_GUIDE.md. Batch meaningful site changes rather than trigger a deployment per research iteration. Do not alter workflows or publish live merely to save a research note.
