## Organ dossier implications

Heart and circulation gain pulse–Old Sun and ethical cognition references; liver and lungs gain carefully separated postmortem-force and pathological-boundary accounts. Kidneys gain three distinct therapeutic mechanisms, not a synthesized invented cure theory. Brain/head gains cavity versus filled-region distinctions, while solar plexus and diaphragm gain historically attributed drug/spiritualization claims. The personal meditation differentiates heart-region light, abdomen-to-head sound and head-to-body life experience. These are instructions or attributed observations, not demonstrated organ transformations.

## Historical claims requiring explicit flags

Incorrect infection accounts, supernatural tuberculosis explanations, universal psychiatric imprint theories, claimed drug-induced cognition, toxic sensory-organ injections, and transgenerational or karmic blame remain clearly marked as unvalidated or harmful. Polemical religious and ethnic characterizations in -12 §2 and §11 are covered as historical context, not adopted explanatory categories. The nitrogen-filled universe story in the supplement is explicitly rejected as nonsense by the speaker; it must not be extracted as an endorsed cosmology.
