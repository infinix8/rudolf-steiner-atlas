## What the original lectures establish within Steiner's conceptual system

1. **Matter as arrested process (Apr 11 §§2–3,19–20).** His original German uses `Vorgang` and `Prozeß`. He distinguishes a substance's mere detected presence from its purported building, changing or eliminative activity. These are Steiner's metaphysical/medical propositions, not independently verified physiology.
2. **Head, chest and limb system are not mutually exclusive owners of physical/etheric/astral/I (Apr 11 §§4–9).** The head allegedly shows a more primary physical process plus etheric/astral/I *imprints* (`Abdruck`); the rhythmic chest a physical–etheric interplay; the limbs metabolism a physical–etheric–astral interplay and dynamic I-imprint. The same members, different relation types and distributions.
3. **Head-to-organ differentiation (Apr 11 §17).** Steiner explicitly mentions the **heart and liver** as recipients of differentiating head-directed formative processes. That is a present-body model, distinct from Old Saturn heart origin or Jupiter/Sun symbolism.
4. **Etheric organization is internally differentiated (Apr 12 §§3–9).** He lists warmth, light, chemical and life ethers and uses the physical differences among stomach, heart and liver to argue against the etheric body as undifferentiated mist. He calls the eye a light-formed organ, without establishing biological eye origin.
5. **Regional sleep rearrangement (Apr 12 §26).** German `Umlagerung`: during sleep I and astral involvement is said to withdraw relatively from **head and respiration**, but become more pronounced in **metabolism and circulation**. This corrects the oversimplification that both members leave the physical organism uniformly.
6. **Constitutional schematic (Apr 12 §§19–25).** I/death, astral/illness, etheric/health, physical/nutrition. This is not a scientifically valid etiology of illness.
7. **Medical safety (Apr 11 §§12–13,18; Apr 12 §§10–12,27–30).** Assertions about plant ash, toxic metals, mineral agents, malnutrition and sleep states are indexed as historical Steiner claims. **Do not translate them into interventions, prescriptions, or diagnostic guidance.**
