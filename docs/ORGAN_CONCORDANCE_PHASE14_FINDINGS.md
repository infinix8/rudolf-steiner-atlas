The central research bridge is explicit: Steiner identifies forces used in higher cognition with formative bodily forces. This supports a typed **cognition–formation relation**, not an inference that ordinary attention to an organ gives conscious creative power. His future-form prediction also needs its own temporal predicate. It cannot be substituted for a present training attainment.
