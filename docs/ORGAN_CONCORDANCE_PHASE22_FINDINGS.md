## Organ evolution and correspondence

[GA316-05 §§12–17](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA316/Compare_05.php) distinguishes head warmth as present continuation of Old Saturn from heart–stem/leaf contemplation evoking Old Sun and metabolic-limb–flower contemplation evoking Old Moon. The latter correspondences do not explicitly date the completed anatomical heart or name its hierarchy builders. [GA316-08 §5](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA316/Compare_08.php) concerns present Earth–Moon construction and Saturn dispersal, distinguished from the ancient-stage framework.

[GA316-07 §§8–9](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA316/Compare_07.php) calls heart and uterus perceptual organs for ordinary and postfertilization circulation and compares their direct/reflected relation with Sun and Moon. This is functional analogy and imaginative correspondence, not automatically an Old Sun/Old Moon origin chronology. The same lecture places unnamed beings in three lower vehicles while locating freedom in I organization (§16); no exclusive organ builders are inferred.

[GA316-01 §5](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA316/Compare_01.php) makes I activity directly thermal and indirectly active throughout the organism. At §15 German Bienenvater is beekeeper: English drone king and its later queen substitution distort the hive analogy. The claimed universal tissue renewal remains medically inaccurate despite correcting the translation.

## Qualifications

GA316-07 distinguishes patient self-healing from alleged interpersonal magnetism and warns about fading ability and charlatanism. Its harmful claim that therapeutic knowledge protects more strongly than smallpox vaccination is explicitly flagged, not used as infection guidance. Hair–iron/sulfur, palmistry, spiritual pathology and lead therapy remain attributed historical claims. GA316-05 §33 again rejects karmic fatalism; -08 §2 insists on healing effort, without turning this into advice for futile or unwanted intervention.
