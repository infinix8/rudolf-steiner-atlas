# Complementary architecture: an index **over** the archives

**Decision (2026-11-01):** The Atlas must complement [Rudolf Steiner Archive](https://rsarchive.org/) and the [Rudolf Steiner Archiv's Complete Works catalogue](https://www.rudolf-steiner.com/en/edition-englisch/complete-works/). It must **not** become a competing text library, a copy of archive pages, or a mirror of their topic indices.

## Different responsibilities

| What readers need | Source archives | Rudolf Steiner Atlas |
|---|---|---|
| Full authored books and lecture transcripts | Primary home for reading | **Links outward**, no full-text hosting |
| Bibliographic authority, GA edition details | Official Complete Works catalogue | Records GA/edition references and redirects outward |
| Browse by title, GA number, date and existing broad topic | Existing archive functions | Links to and **bridges** these existing functions |
| Find precise concepts within and across disciplinary areas | Not the main navigation strategy of topic lists | Hierarchical classification with stable IDs |
| Follow the same idea across medicine, nature, pedagogy, spirituality | Requires manual work | Typed cross-domain links, marked editorial until documented |
| Discover a teaching by related question or alternate terminology | Full-text search and source browsing | Concept/alias search, multilingual terms (planned) |
| Know exact provenance of one conceptual claim | Available in full original context | Brief source records, verification status, original page link |
| Understand variations between lectures/editions | Full primary texts and editorial documentation | Comparison map and translation notes (planned), no reproduction |

**Independent organizations:** The US-based Steiner Online Library/Rudolf Steiner Archive (rsarchive.org) and the Rudolf Steiner Archiv in Dornach (rudolf-steiner.com) are *distinct institutions*. Do not imply affiliation, funding, sponsorship or endorsement. The archive itself states this distinction.

## Five layers of the Atlas

1. **Concept topology:** stable, granular canonical topics. V1 contains 6 regions, 26 domains and 1,006 nodes.
2. **Synonyms / alternate paths:** multiple entry points into the *same* concept, including translated or changing terminology. Do not duplicate nodes to create a second explanation of the same idea.
3. **Semantic relationships:** labelled links; separate archival evidence from editorial inference. Do not mistake an analogy for identity or a shared mention for a causal teaching.
4. **Evidence gateways:** cite exact chapter or dated lecture when located; retain bibliographic volume-only leads clearly marked as leads. The reader opens the authoritative source.
5. **Discovery interface:** search, breadcrumbs, topic trails and later timelines, comparison maps, graph visualization and source coverage views.

## Strict non-duplication policy

**Do not:**
- Copy or republish Steiner books, chapter transcripts, lecture texts, translations, or archive topic inventories in this repository.
- Bulk scrape, mirror, download, crawl or automate extraction from rsarchive.org. Its June 2026 Terms restrict automated access, text/data mining and republication; obtain explicit permission for any future systematic content ingestion.
- Present any of the Atlas's 14 broad archive topic links as a replacement catalogue.
- Treat the source host's book blurbs or a later writer's interpretation as Steiner's own words.
- Imply source partnership or official status.

**Do:**
- Store our own original taxonomy labels, short original editorial concept descriptions, associations, stable IDs and outward URLs.
- Record volume IDs / lecture dates / translation variants and exact source page links.
- Provide source-site attribution alongside links.
- Verify and preserve URLs when adding passage-level evidence, including edition and translation identity.
- Keep unconfirmed mappings explicitly identified as editor-created research hypotheses.

## Evidence and distinction model

- **Unmapped:** concept without source evidence.
- **Lead:** GA volume/series might be relevant; no exact passage established.
- **Located:** exact linked chapter or lecture page found and opened; still no exhaustive passage review.
- **Reviewed:** source passage read in context, edition/translation recorded, faithful original paraphrase and relationship checked.
- **Contested:** attribution, translation, historicity or editorial interpretation unresolved.

The existing v1 taxonomy uses some broad GA links and links labelled 'anchor evidence'. Their labels do **not** constitute fully reviewed passages. The separate research registry specifies located-but-unreviewed external links.

## Editorial reading journeys

A study path is a proposed way of reading across topics; a path is **not** a lecture series composed by Steiner. Each step should lead to canonical concept nodes and then to original sources. The Atlas should display the uncertainty and avoid declaring a cross-domain connection *proven* until passages support it.

## Safety and critical history

Steiner's spiritual, historical and scientific claims must remain attributed as Steiner's views; do not state them as independently established. Medical and educational descriptions are historical research, not instructions replacing licensed care or contemporary educational safeguarding. Preserve, contextualize and critically describe discriminatory or racialized passages rather than normalizing or silently deleting them.

## Design tests for new features

A feature belongs in the Atlas if it materially improves **findability, navigability, interconnection or provenance**, without copying source material. If it simply makes another reading list or duplicates full text, prefer linking to the original archive.
