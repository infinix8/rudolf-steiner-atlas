## Three different planetary questions

[I10–13](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/English/SOL/19090412a01.html) distinguishes physical planets, spiritual realms designated by planetary names, and modes of human seeing. Steiner attributes changing perception from alleged ancient clairvoyant planetary auras toward present physical sight; the aura is said to remain although ordinary eyes no longer perceive it. This is a claimed spiritual history, not verified archaeology or optical physiology.

I13 links Moon/Angels, Mercury/Archangels, Venus/Archai, Sun/Exusiai, Mars/Dynamis, Jupiter/Kyriotetes and Saturn/Thrones. [German13](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/Compare_01.php) confirms the terms. Latin names remain essential because English Powers and related labels vary and can obscure rank distinctions. I13's sphere mapping is a different predicate from [III4](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/English/SOL/19090413a01.html), where Angels undergo their own humanity stage on Old Moon, Archangels on Old Sun and Archai on Old Saturn.

III2 expressly distinguishes those Old planetary embodiments from today's Moon, Sun and Saturn. Thus Mercury/Archangels as a sphere relationship is not contradicted merely by Old Sun/Archangels as the beings' former humanity stage. Their subject and temporal frame differ. Neither statement alone tells which being constructed a particular human organ.

This matters when joining GA128 VIII's organ/planet relationships. Heart/Sun and Sun/Exusiai do not by themselves prove an exclusive Exusiai builder of the heart. Spleen/Saturn and Saturn/Thrones likewise do not supply a spleen-specific origin date. The atlas now stores the general constitutional statements in a whole-human-organization dossier, preserving the distinction from individual anatomical organs. There are40 dossiers in total, including systems and this constitutional dossier; this is not a count of40 distinct anatomical organs.

## Heat and human participation

[II4–5](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/English/SOL/19090412p01.html) treats warmth as both outwardly perceived and inwardly felt, a gateway between material and soul. This provides a comparison with GA128 VIII41–44's blood-warmth-to-compassion account, but II does not itself supply that later blood-specific mechanism. II7's assertion that physical eyes never see light is retained as his historical optical thesis, not accepted as a modern account of retinal phototransduction or direct luminous sources.

II11–21 describes elemental messengers entering human beings through perception and being bound or liberated through human cognitive, aesthetic, active, emotional and spiritual conduct. Four sequences concern fire/smoke, day/night, lunar waxing/waning and seasonal summer/winter. The asserted fate at death belongs to the elementals. [German II15](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/Compare_02.php) explicitly excludes the human I as the immediate referent of the Gita passage discussed in I15–16. Consequently, the literal calendar-death interpretation introduced in I is not the course's final reading. These are Steiner's interpretations of another religious text; they are not independently verified claims about what the Gita originally means.

The ethical cosmic responsibility account must not be converted into blame for fatigue, disability or mental illness. Terms of laziness, discontent and hypochondria remain attributable historical language. No disease mechanism or therapeutic action is established by them.

## Substance donation differs from form and consciousness

III5–12 describes Saturn warmth formations, Archai self-consciousness through resistance, Thrones dissolution of the external remainder into planetary night, and Archangel gas/fire/light embodiment on Old Sun. The agent changes with the predicate. A breathing image on Saturn occurs before air; it therefore does not supply a literal primordial lung. Old Sun gas-breathing describes Archangel/planetary processes, not necessarily the anatomical origin of present human lungs.

IV7–13 sharpens agency. Archai can shape warmth but cannot provide its substance. Thrones donate warmth-fire from their own being; Archai form and individualize it; Exusiai, Dynamis and Kyriotetes assist the regulated activity. German alongside the English confirms these distinctions. A graph edge simply saying all these beings create the body would discard the course's explicit division of work.

IV16–22 assigns Cherubim reception and support of outward-spreading Archangels, while their influence on dark solar gas forms animal prototypes. IV19 compares the human physical foundation in Saturn/Thrones sacrifice with the animal physical foundation on Sun/Cherubim. These are different kingdoms and stages. They do not directly allocate human heart, liver or lungs to Cherubim. The zodiac figure narrative is indexed as cosmological explanation rather than accepted zoological ancestry.

## Human transformation and creative giving

IV8 separately describes I transformation of astral into Spirit Self/Manas, etheric into Life Spirit/Budhi and physical into Spirit Man/Atma. The physical vehicle is said still to appear physical outwardly while inwardly governed by the I. This is not already a disappearance of physical anatomy or a detailed transformation of a named organ. IV9 places the first physical human seed on Old Saturn and Earth I-reception later, then describes development toward cosmic giving. The taking-to-giving theme supplies a precise general future-creative framework to compare with GA128's compassion account; no exact organ metamorphosis is invented where the source is silent.

## Remaining questions

Later GA110 lectures and both Q&A entries remain unread in this first-pass ledger. Their qualifications, including planetary terminology, must be checked before final course synthesis. Steiner's identification of the Dionysian hierarchy teacher with Paul's pupil is retained as his historical attribution, not silently adopted as verified authorship of the later Dionysian corpus. Existing organ-surface, sleep, renal-intervention, arsenic-scope and translation issues remain open. This phase adds carefully typed cosmic context without claiming to have solved the individual organ histories.
