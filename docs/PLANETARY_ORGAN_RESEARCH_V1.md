# Planetary realms and the human organs: source crosswalk, first dedicated audit
**Rudolf Steiner Atlas · Phase 3 · 19 November 2026**

> **Scope and verification:** Seven explicitly named organ-to-planet correspondences from GA 107 §13; checks against GA 128 lectures II, IV and VIII; separate supporting passages in GA 145, GA 209, GA 218 and GA 239. Primary sources are original Steiner lectures available in English; this is **not** a full GA 107 corpus review, original-language collation, independent historical corroboration or biomedical evidence.

## Central distinction

The seven organ names below are *occult/etheric images* employed by Steiner, **not exclusive planet-to-organ construction assignments**, not a list of actual energy inputs to organs, not established anatomical functions, and not clinical recommendations. GA 128 Lecture II §11 initially calls the analogies provisional and asks the audience not to assume that the shared names demonstrate an external connection. GA 128 VIII develops a claimed systematic relationship, but this does not establish the physiological mechanism.

**Avoid a common attribution mistake:** the GA 126 *Occult History* Appendix reproduces a historical Tycho Brahe formulation of a similar organ/planet list. The proof text for Steiner's own full sevenfold enumeration is **GA 107 VIII §13**, not the quoted Tycho passage.

## Seven research dossiers

### Saturn — Spleen
**Evidence class:** explicit etheric/occult organ analogy. **Organ Atlas:** [Spleen](../atlas/organ-detail.html?organ=organ-spleen&layer=interpretation). **Planet Atlas:** [saturn](../atlas/planetary-sphere.html?sphere=saturn).

**Steiner's image:** The first of the three inward organs of the ascending nourishment process.

Steiner treats the spleen as an 'inner Saturn' in GA 128. He begins cautiously, asking listeners to understand the planetary name as an image for the external cosmos being reflected in internal organic activity. The association is named again when he describes a coordinated inner world system rather than a set of isolated organ functions.

In GA 128 VIII, the spleen joins the liver and gallbladder on the side of the inner planetary system related to the heart. The lesson is about an alleged pattern of relations between systems, not a scientifically supported effect of Saturn's rings or a single Saturn hierarchy manufacturing the spleen.

**Medical and textual caution:** Lead appears as a historical mineral correspondence in GA 128 VIII. Lead exposure is harmful; the teaching is not treatment guidance.

**Source readings:** [GA 107 §13, 1908-11-10](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA107/English/SOL/19081110p01.html) · [GA 128 §11, 1911-03-21](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/English/SOL/19110321p01.html) · [GA 128 §§18–23, 1911-03-28](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/English/SOL/19110328p01.html).

### Jupiter — Liver
**Evidence class:** explicit etheric/occult organ analogy. **Organ Atlas:** [Liver](../atlas/organ-detail.html?organ=organ-liver&layer=interpretation). **Planet Atlas:** [jupiter](../atlas/planetary-sphere.html?sphere=jupiter).

**Steiner's image:** An inner Jupiter within the larger blood-and-nutrition picture.

GA 128 II §11 speaks of the liver as an 'inner Jupiter,' beside Saturn/spleen and Mars/gallbladder, at the point where Steiner introduces the notion of outer cosmic activity contracting into an inner organ world. His own wording initially presents the naming as a hypothetical analogy that subsequent lectures explore.

GA 128 VIII develops the image of the liver in relation to the heart, spleen and gallbladder. This symbolic/etheric correspondence is separate from other organ-corpus claims about the liver's role in will, memory or development. It does not imply that Jupiter alone builds the finished liver.

**Medical and textual caution:** Steiner also assigns tin as a historical metal analogue. Do not infer a therapeutic indication.

**Source readings:** [GA 107 §13, 1908-11-10](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA107/English/SOL/19081110p01.html) · [GA 128 §11, 1911-03-21](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/English/SOL/19110321p01.html) · [GA 128 §§18–23, 1911-03-28](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/English/SOL/19110328p01.html).

### Mars — Gallbladder
**Evidence class:** explicit etheric/occult organ analogy. **Organ Atlas:** [Gallbladder](../atlas/organs.html?organ=organ-gallbladder). **Planet Atlas:** [mars](../atlas/planetary-sphere.html?sphere=mars).

**Steiner's image:** The Mars analogy in the inward liver–gallbladder–spleen constellation.

In GA 128 II Steiner calls the gallbladder an 'inner Mars' as part of a schematic description of digestive and blood-related processes. In GA 128 VIII it appears alongside Jupiter/liver and Saturn/spleen on one side of the heart-centred inner planetary system.

That is a specific occult organ naming, not an equation between all activities of Mars, the Dynamis and this one anatomical organ. Other discussions of Mars as cosmic movement or speech concern a separate conceptual level.

**Medical and textual caution:** GA 128 VIII also mentions iron as a supposed counterbalancing substance. No treatment or supplement guidance follows from the historical claim.

**Source readings:** [GA 107 §13, 1908-11-10](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA107/English/SOL/19081110p01.html) · [GA 128 §11, 1911-03-21](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/English/SOL/19110321p01.html) · [GA 128 §§18–23, 1911-03-28](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/English/SOL/19110328p01.html).

### Sun — Heart
**Evidence class:** explicit etheric/occult organ analogy. **Organ Atlas:** [Heart](../atlas/organ-detail.html?organ=organ-heart&layer=interpretation). **Planet Atlas:** [sun](../atlas/planetary-sphere.html?sphere=sun).

**Steiner's image:** The centre around which the inner planetary system is presented.

In GA 128 VIII Steiner compares the heart to the Sun at the centre of an inner world system: liver/Jupiter, gallbladder/Mars and spleen/Saturn form one group of relationships, while lungs/Mercury and kidneys/Venus form another. He uses this to picture the interdependence of processes in his occult physiology.

GA 145 §21 again treats the heart's activity as a microcosmic picture of solar processes. These are esoteric conceptions; some associated statements in GA 145 about the heart and circulation conflict with established cardiovascular physiology and are not presented here as medical fact.

**Medical and textual caution:** Gold is mentioned as a historical material correspondence. The spiritual Sun is also distinct from the Old Sun evolutionary condition and from the Christ mystery.

**Source readings:** [GA 107 §13, 1908-11-10](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA107/English/SOL/19081110p01.html) · [GA 128 §§18–23, 1911-03-28](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/English/SOL/19110328p01.html) · [GA 145 §§21–23, 1913-03-21](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA145/English/SOL/19130321p01.html).

### Venus — Kidneys
**Evidence class:** explicit etheric/occult organ analogy. **Organ Atlas:** [Kidneys](../atlas/organ-detail.html?organ=organ-kidneys&layer=interpretation). **Planet Atlas:** [venus](../atlas/planetary-sphere.html?sphere=venus).

**Steiner's image:** The Venus system mediating the inner and outer processes in GA 128.

Steiner assigns the kidney system to Venus while contrasting the inwardly directed organ activities with the pulmonary relation to the surrounding air. GA 128 IV describes the kidney system as situated between the opposing sets of processes that act upon the blood.

GA 128 VIII reprises Venus/kidneys in the image of the heart/Sun and the nearer planetary organs. The attribution concerns a claimed etheric system of relations; it does not make Venus the sole author of the kidneys' anatomical form.

**Medical and textual caution:** Copper is a historical correspondence in GA 128 VIII; no evidence of medical benefit is thereby established.

**Source readings:** [GA 107 §13, 1908-11-10](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA107/English/SOL/19081110p01.html) · [GA 128 §§12–14, 20, 1911-03-23](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/English/SOL/19110323p01.html) · [GA 128 §§18–23, 1911-03-28](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/English/SOL/19110328p01.html).

### Mercury — Lungs
**Evidence class:** explicit etheric/occult organ analogy. **Organ Atlas:** [Lungs](../atlas/organ-detail.html?organ=organ-lungs&layer=interpretation). **Planet Atlas:** [mercury](../atlas/planetary-sphere.html?sphere=mercury).

**Steiner's image:** A pulmonary 'Mercury' in relation to external air and internal blood.

In GA 128 IV Steiner names the lung system Mercury. He characterizes breathing as a contact between the outside world and the blood, contrasted with the processes of internal nourishment represented by other planetary organ names.

GA 128 VIII links lungs/Mercury and kidneys/Venus to the other side of a Sun/heart-centred organic picture. It is a historical esoteric model, not an explanation of respiratory physiology or an instruction to use mercury compounds.

**Medical and textual caution:** Metallic mercury and many mercury compounds are toxic. The historic metal association must never be construed as treatment advice.

**Source readings:** [GA 107 §13, 1908-11-10](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA107/English/SOL/19081110p01.html) · [GA 128 §§12–14, 20, 1911-03-23](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/English/SOL/19110323p01.html) · [GA 128 §§18–23, 1911-03-28](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/English/SOL/19110328p01.html).

### Moon — Brain
**Evidence class:** explicit etheric/occult organ analogy. **Organ Atlas:** [Brain](../atlas/organ-detail.html?organ=organ-brain&layer=interpretation). **Planet Atlas:** [moon](../atlas/planetary-sphere.html?sphere=moon).

**Steiner's image:** The Moon image concerns the brain, with an important wider-cosmos qualification.

GA 107 VIII §13 expressly names the brain as Moon, completing the sevenfold organ image. The later GA 128 inner-organ cluster principally develops the other six correspondences; it does not make the brain simply another abdominal 'inner planet.'

GA 145 §22 describes a closer relation of brain substance to the Moon but simultaneously places the brain in connection with the wider starry cosmos and distinguishes it from the heart's solar relations. This prevents a simplistic one-planet-one-organ causal interpretation.

**Medical and textual caution:** The checked passage set does not support assigning a corresponding metal to brain/Moon in the same GA 128 metal list.

**Source readings:** [GA 107 §13, 1908-11-10](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA107/English/SOL/19081110p01.html) · [GA 145 §§21–23, 1913-03-21](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA145/English/SOL/19130321p01.html).


## Four broader frameworks that must not be overlaid as a single map

### 1. The heart-centred inner planetary system — Occult physiology of bodily functions
Steiner compares the heart/Sun with two groups of organ processes. Liver/Jupiter, gallbladder/Mars and spleen/Saturn belong to one inward stream; lung/Mercury and kidney/Venus to a different stream. The brain/Moon appears in the sevenfold enumeration of GA 107, but the six-organ system in GA 128 must not be mistaken for the whole planetary hierarchy map.

**Passage(s):** [GA 128 §11](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/English/SOL/19110321p01.html) · [GA 128 §§12–14, 20](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/English/SOL/19110323p01.html) · [GA 128 §§18–23](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/English/SOL/19110328p01.html). **Type:** organ-system.

### 2. Consciousness, metabolism and rhythmic mediation — General human organization
In GA 209 §31 the outer three planetary influences are associated with a consciousness pole, the inner three with metabolism and limbs, and the Sun with rhythmic mediation. This is a body-systems scheme, not a second organ-by-organ assignment; for example, it does not contradict or erase the particular Saturn/spleen symbolic image in GA 128.

**Passage(s):** [GA 209 §31](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA209/English/APC1948/19211124p01.html). **Type:** systemic-polarity.

### 3. Senses and interior life as opposing fields — Spiritual beings and human development
GA 218 §§25–31 attributes activity concerning external sense organs to Saturn/Jupiter/Mars beings and the inner bodily constitution to Moon/Mercury/Venus. The subject is a spiritual struggle around human organization, rather than a claim that the lungs or brain should be reassigned to different planets.

**Passage(s):** [GA 218 §§25–31](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA218/English/SOL/19221116p01.html). **Type:** other-framework.

### 4. The cosmic body experienced after death — Life between death and rebirth
GA 239 §2 depicts a human being in the Sun sphere experiencing Moon/Mercury/Venus as limbs, Sun as rhythmic system and heart/lungs, and Mars/Jupiter/Saturn as a cosmic head. That is a symbolic experience in the post-mortem spiritual world—not a competing physical-organ table.

**Passage(s):** [GA 239 §2](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA239/English/SOL/19240525p01.html). **Type:** after-death-picture.


## How planetary hierarchy knowledge differs from this crosswalk

The GA 110 hierarchy-boundary scheme connects Moon to Angels, Mercury to Archangels, Venus to Archai, Sun to Spirits of Form, Mars to Spirits of Movement, Jupiter to Spirits of Wisdom, and Saturn to Thrones. It names spiritual regions and functions. **It does not say each named hierarchy is the sole maker of the organ corresponding to its planet in GA 107**.

Similarly, the GA 237 names of planetary Archangel-intelligences refer to administrative functions, not organ morphology. The GA 209 and GA 218 polarity schemes concern entire body systems; the GA 239 Sun-existence figure describes a post-mortem spiritual world. Charts combining them must label the lecture context and relation predicate.

## What cannot responsibly be said about Vulcan

The reviewed GA 107 and GA 128 organ correspondences do not contain an eighth item for Vulcan. The secret Atlantean Vulcan Oracle (GA 13/109), modern Vulcan beings (GA 204), and future cosmic Vulcan are other types of teaching. Neither their existence in Steiner's system nor a general Earth-oriented emphasis justifies inventing a 'Vulcan organ'. Absence from these selected source views is not proof of absence throughout his corpus; leave the question open.

## Historical medical caution

GA 128 VIII §§21–22 relates the inner organ imagery to gold, tin, iron, lead, mercury and copper. Steiner uses these in speculative therapeutic argument. **This project is not prescribing metals, herbs, extracts, or treatments; exposure to lead and mercury is potentially harmful.** These lecture claims have not been established as medically accurate. Likewise, GA 145 includes assertions about the heart's mechanics inconsistent with established cardiovascular physiology. The Atlas explains the historical teaching, not evidence-based treatment.
