## The strongest development connection: morphology and meditation

GA 317 X §§21–23 and XI §34 explicitly connect a point–circle reversal to the head and metabolic-limb human being. X §22 extends this to the spinal cord, jaw metamorphosis and tooth/toe polarity. These passages add a **methodological relation** to the structural arrangement already indexed from V §§3–6: the I-point/head and I-circumference/limbs are now considered through meditative reversal. This is more specific than an editorial suggestion to visualize any organ.

X §23's inward-creation language belongs in this meditative context. It supports a claim about inward reconstruction or apprehension of form within Steiner's method; it does not demonstrate physical manufacture of an organ. Likewise, XI §34 connects the experience of reversal to a felt passage from head to metabolism, without providing a clinical test of that experience. The distinction between a schematic morphology, an inward exercise and a claimed therapeutic mechanism must remain intact.

XII §§9–10 then supplies an explicit developmental frame: the physical, etheric and astral organizations have undergone four, three and two transformations, while the present I remains in becoming. Its inter-incarnation account of the I is a metaphysical statement, not a diagnosis that ordinary adults are psychologically infantile. The same passage points back to the prerequisites in GA 10. Those prerequisites still require their own complete primary-source audit before the Atlas could offer a systematic training concordance.

## Ongoing spiritual agency without an exclusive organ builder

IX §§27–30 identifies language beings said to have developed Spirit-Self more distinctly than human beings. Their activity through speech and eurythmy is a direct statement about **ongoing agency**, and therefore relevant to the question whether spiritual beings remain active in human organization. But the functional designation does not identify a named hierarchy rank, nor does the passage assign one such being as the exclusive maker of the larynx.

This qualifies the earlier GA 313 IX cognition–formation evidence. Both sources link human activity with supersensible formative agency, yet their predicates differ: one discusses cognitive forces and organ formation; the other concerns language, pedagogy and Spirit-Self. Shared words such as “intuition” cannot erase that difference. No new one-to-one angel/organ table follows.

## New organ evidence and case qualifications

VIII §§8–11 locates head formation within a purported cosmic uterine enclosure and contrasts it with later limb orientation. The same case adds speculative paternal-limb and maternal-head inheritance, a heart/joint-fluid association, and maternal emotional causation. These are not measured genetic or embryological findings. The record separates supplied history from the causal interpretation Steiner places upon it.

IX §§10,18 and XI §9 considerably expand the pituitary dossier: different cases and routes are assigned counter-deforming, harmonizing or centrifugal growth effects. These formulations should be compared as a family of asserted formative actions, not merged into a validated endocrine mechanism. XI §§1–2 adds the bladder/nasopharyngeal counterpart and physical/etheric memory account. New bladder, nasopharynx, spinal-cord and ears dossiers are intentionally sparse source indexes; their cosmic histories remain open.

XII §§4–6 adds a differentiated account of lactation. Forces are said to move from the maternal metabolic-limb region to the rhythmic region, losing I-content while retaining astral content. Milk's astrality is related to that directed toward blossoms, expressly without complete identity. This refines the older sensory-metamorphosis account of milk rather than simply repeating it. XII §12's heart/kidney coordination is an institutional analogy; it supplies no new cosmic origin or organ builder.

## German findings and unresolved contradictions

The [verification log](../research/organ-german-checks-and-textual-issues-v17.json) contains **10 selective check entries**. Significant results:

- VIII §18 has a sulfuric constitutional description; SOL's “spirited” hides that technical claim.
- VIII §16 refers to outward temples and purported astral organ regions. IX §10 does explicitly name temporal lobes in German. The anatomical assertion therefore cannot be dismissed merely as a translator replacing “temples” with “lobes”; its unsupported diagnostic inference must remain visible.
- XI §17's prose assigns Venus to sentient soul and Mars to intellectual soul. The following table reverses those two soul pairs. The disagreement occurs in German as well as English. Both mappings remain recorded as an **apparent conflict**, pending edition/manuscript comparison.
- IX §20's German acknowledges the lack of reduced head circumference before criticizing other treatment approaches. SOL obscures that qualification. Claims of successful treatment must not be inferred from general therapeutic confidence.
- Previously logged pituitary-direction, Balkenstich, sleep-distribution and diarrhea issues remain unresolved.

X–XI's astrological albinism theory is treated as unsupported disease explanation. Two charts, uncertain birth information and heuristic language do not establish a population-level causal relation. Wrong claims about sulfur/iron, head shape, morality, astral disease and maternal behavior remain marked. Lead, arsenic, nicotine and other historical proposals are not therapeutic guidance; procedural dosing is omitted. Coercive behavioral regimens and minimizing crises are preserved as problematic historical statements, not normalized as care.
