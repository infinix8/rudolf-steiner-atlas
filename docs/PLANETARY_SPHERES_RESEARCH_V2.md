## Important correction from GA 136
GA 110 VI uses a shorthand hierarchy-to-sphere sequence (Moon Angeloi, Mercury Archangeloi, Venus Archai, Sun Exusiai, Mars Dynamis, Jupiter Kyriotetes, Saturn Thrones). GA 136 V, **§§15–17**, says the **outward form of a physical planet** expresses the work of a Spirit of Form; what surrounds it is the work of multiple spiritual orders. These two claims are not equal one-to-one classifications. A planetary sphere should not be presented as having exactly one kind of resident being. Nor is a name such as Michael/solar intelligence in GA 237 XI the same as the whole solar Exusiai realm.

## Theological and historical caution

GA 121 VI also contains Steiner's racially essentialist evolutionary classification. It does **not** represent modern biology or an acceptable account of human populations. The Atlas cites only the six-Elohim and lunar-Elohim passages pertinent to this research, and does not replicate or endorse his racial categories. Readers following the original can encounter that historical material.

## Three types of planetary names
1. **Present spiritual sphere:** Moon, Mercury, Venus, Sun, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn in GA 110 VI; boundaries of domains, not observable surfaces.
2. **Large cosmic embodiment:** Old Saturn, Old Sun, Old Moon, Earth, future Jupiter, future Venus, future Vulcan; GA 96 explicitly disallows equating these directly with today's planets.
3. **Oracle / planetary intelligence:** Atlantean initiatory stream in GA 13/109, or Archangelic planetary intelligence in GA 237. These are different categories and require separate source tracing.
4. **Vulcan exception:** a secret Atlantean Oracle, a later claim about Vulcan beings, and the projected future cosmic Vulcan. No additional securely documented present hierarchy-boundary sphere equivalent to the seven in GA 110 VI.

## Twenty-four added evidence notes — inventory
### Saturn
- **The spiritual past is not a physical signal** (Direct passage) — GA 228 contrasts Saturn's remembrance of cosmic history with Jupiter's living thought. The passage concerns spiritually perceived beings, not radiation from the ringed planet. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA228/English/SOL/19230727p01.html
- **Old Saturn is a different referent** (Direct passage) — In GA 96 Steiner explicitly warns against identifying Earth's earlier Saturn condition with a human journey to the present astronomical Saturn. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA096/English/SOL/19070304p01.html
- **A complete planet has several hierarchies** (Cross-lecture synthesis) — GA 136 depicts the planet's outer form as associated with Spirits of Form, while many other hierarchies form its supersensible 'envelopes'; the GA 110 Saturn/Thrones correlation is therefore not exclusive. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA136/English/SOL/19120407p01.html

### Jupiter
- **What Jupiter adds to thought** (Direct passage) — Steiner calls Jupiter a 'thinker' that represents the cosmic present in living ideas, set against Saturn's historical remembrance. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA228/English/SOL/19230727p01.html
- **One task can change over time** (Direct passage) — In GA 102 §25 he cautions that the work of planetary beings changes through evolution, citing Jupiter beings' earlier etheric-body contributions and later role in the consciousness soul. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA102/English/SOL/19080215p01.html
- **Future Jupiter is not today's planet** (Direct passage) — GA 96 §9 expressly distinguishes the future Jupiter embodiment of Earth evolution from the visible planet bearing the same name. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA096/English/SOL/19070304p01.html

### Mars
- **Mars and soul formation** (Direct passage) — GA 102 §§23–24 assigns an activity of Mars beings in the formation of the sentient or feeling soul; it is not a universal law of modern psychology. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA102/English/SOL/19080215p01.html
- **Speech is not the same as motion** (Cross-lecture synthesis) — GA 231 presents Mars as a realm of the cosmic Word, a spiritual experience between incarnations. GA 110 instead uses Mars to mark the Dynamis' sphere boundary. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA231/English/SOL/19231117p01.html
- **A planet is not a single god** (Cross-lecture synthesis) — GA 136 describes planets as manifestations of a Spirit of Form surrounded by further hierarchies; Mars/Dynamis in GA 110 names one boundary within this wider model. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA136/English/SOL/19120407p01.html

### Sun
- **Six solar Elohim and one lunar Elohim** (Direct passage) — GA 121 §§3–5 describes six Spirits of Form acting from the Sun and Jehovah/Jahve's cooperating activity through the Moon, in order to sustain conditions for human development. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA121/English/SOL/19100612a01.html
- **Michael's intelligence is a separate level** (Cross-lecture synthesis) — GA 237 §§13–15 associates Michael with the solar intelligence and overall coordination of planetary intelligences; GA 110 assigns the Sun-sphere boundary to Exusiai. The sources refer to different functions. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA237/English/SOL/19240808p01.html
- **The Sun Oracle and hidden Vulcan** (Direct passage) — GA 109 §§9–11 describes the Sun Oracle as central to Atlantean initiation while Vulcan secretly prepared a future task; this is distinct from the current planetary sphere map. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA109/English/AP1986/19090531p01.html

### Venus
- **Spirit-Self and the consciousness soul** (Direct passage) — GA 102 §24 associates Venus beings with the activity of Spirit-Self entering the consciousness soul, distinct from Jupiter's related contribution. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA102/English/SOL/19080215p01.html
- **Why the hierarchy map is not exclusive** (Cross-lecture synthesis) — GA 136 §16 says the outer form of any planet expresses the activity of Spirits of Form while its 'envelopes' contain other hierarchies. Thus GA 110's Venus/Archai placement is a region boundary, not a monopoly. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA136/English/SOL/19120407p01.html
- **Old names can switch Mercury and Venus** (Direct passage) — GA 110 VII discusses an older naming convention at variance with present astronomical usage. Different passages should be read in their own historical terminology. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/English/SOL/19090416p01.html

### Mercury
- **Two functions, one planetary stream** (Direct passage) — GA 102 §§22–25 says Mercury beings formed human intellectual-soul capacities and also instructed initiates. Steiner himself calls this a double activity. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA102/English/SOL/19080215p01.html
- **Raphael and the archangelic hierarchy** (Cross-lecture synthesis) — GA 237 lists Raphael with Mercury's planetary intelligence. GA 110 separately places the Archangeloi sphere boundary at Mercury. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA237/English/SOL/19240808p01.html
- **Beware the naming shift** (Direct passage) — The older esoteric interchange of Mercury and Venus discussed in GA 110 VII can change how planetary correspondences are described in different translations and lectures. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/English/SOL/19090416p01.html

### Moon
- **Lunar Jehovah is not an Angel** (Cross-lecture synthesis) — In GA 121 §§3–5 Jehovah/Jahve is one of the seven Elohim, or Spirits of Form, acting through the Moon; this does not contradict GA 110's Moon boundary for Angeloi. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA121/English/SOL/19100612a01.html
- **Moon as spiritual threshold** (Direct passage) — GA 110 VI places individual Angels' domain up to the Moon; the sphere is not simply its observable rocky surface. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/English/SOL/19090415p01.html
- **Three meanings of Moon** (Cross-lecture synthesis) — GA 96 differentiates Old Moon in the sevenfold cosmic development from today's astronomical Moon; the Atlantean Moon Oracle belongs to yet another type of teaching. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA096/English/SOL/19070304p01.html

### Vulcan
- **The secret Atlantean oracle** (Direct passage) — GA 109 §§9–11 mentions Vulcan preparing in secrecy beside the central Sun Oracle. It does not make Vulcan an additional ordinary planet in GA 110's seven hierarchy-boundary sequence. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA109/English/AP1986/19090531p01.html
- **An Earth-oriented initiatory mystery** (Direct passage) — GA 13 IV connects Vulcan's stream with attention to earthly phenomena and the beginnings of arts and sciences. The term Oracle here denotes an initiation current, not a newly discovered physical planet. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/AP1972/GA013_c04.html
- **Modern beings and future world must stay separate** (Cross-lecture synthesis) — GA 204 speaks about Vulcan beings in our own age; GA 96 independently places a future Vulcan condition at the end of a whole cosmic cycle. The two statements have different referents and cannot be reduced to one astronomical object. Sources: https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA096/English/SOL/19070304p01.html

## Limitations / next research
This is a curated English-language passage layer, not all of GA 110, all medical sources or all relevant GA volumes. No German variants independently checked. Paragraph notes refer to Archive passage numbering but do not imply deep-link anchors unless verified. Before making definitive organ-specific planetary charts, audit the full relevant GA 313–317 corpus and avoid medical validation claims. A source card does not prove uniqueness or causal primacy of the linked activity. The Vulcan Oracle / Vulcan beings / future Vulcan are not asserted to be identical.
