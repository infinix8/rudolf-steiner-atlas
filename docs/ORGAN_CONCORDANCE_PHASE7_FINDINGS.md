## 2. A new explanatory axis: from this life's organ to the next life's head (GA 205)

Steiner distinguishes two supposed activities in the same organ:
- **Surface reflection:** the outer surfaces of inner organs reflect current mental or emotional experience back into memory. In §5, the lung surface is connected to representations of external objects and abstract memories; the liver surface especially to emotionally colored remembrance.
- **Interior storage / future formation:** the deeper, retained forces in an organ allegedly pass through death and become formative powers in another incarnation. In §§7, 10, 12 and 14–15 Steiner separates four organ functions.

| Present organ-system | This-life activity Steiner asserts | Next-life formative activity he asserts | Source sections |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lungs** | External-object thought content, abstract representations; surface participates in memory | Forces contribute to the **external physiognomy of the future head** | GA 205 §§5, 7–9 |
| **Liver** | Emotion-colored memory; interior interpreted as reservoir of inward cognitive tendencies | Forces contribute to the **inner disposition of the future brain** | §§5, 10–11 |
| **Kidneys** | Enduring emotional habits and temperament | Forces contribute to **emotional/temperamental predisposition** in the next head organization | §12 |
| **Heart** | Conscience reflected at its surface; moral valuation of actions | Forces carry **karmic predispositions** into the next life | §§13–16, 18–19 |

These are **four different predicates**, not one shared organ-to-planet correspondence. Nor does GA 205 assign one uniquely named spiritual builder to each organ. Its emphasis is on continuity across incarnations. In §15 Steiner groups them together as participating, via the present metabolic-limb organization, in the next incarnation's head.

### Second set of correspondences in the same lecture

In GA 205 §21 Steiner associates **lungs with earth, liver with water, kidneys with air, and heart with warmth**. These are **elemental associations**, not the planetary correspondence scheme in GA 107/128 (Sun/heart, Jupiter/liver, etc.) nor historical cosmic-stage claims. The index must expose **relationship-type filters** to prevent false contradictions.

### Critical correction

In GA 205 §13 Steiner denies that the heart pumps blood. **This is false**: cardiac pumping and circulatory physiology are directly established by modern medical evidence. Likewise, GA 205 §§9–12 and §§18–22 propose unsupported etiologies for intrusive thoughts, hallucinations, diseases and karmic conditions; preserve them as *historical source assertions* only.

## 3. Anatomy as metamorphosis (GA 315)

GA 315 §4 states an unusually useful **general method**: an organ is not an isolated object but a metamorphic variation of other organs and a member of the human whole. In §§5–8 Steiner takes the **larynx** as a concrete example, interpreting it as an **inverted posterior head and rib cage** through etheric formative forces. In §7 he extends the analogy to the **thyroid**, which he characterizes as a metamorphosed forebrain and assigns a chest-related thinking role.

His claims in §§8–12 connect breathing, hearing, laryngeal movement, the head's quieting action, circulation, and the transformation of thought into speech rhythm. This is a different research relation from literal phylogenetic descent or from a named hierarchy 'creating' the larynx.

In §19 Steiner even generalizes that an inner organ's form could be counteracted through an external movement form, giving the gallbladder as an example. This is a **historical therapeutic proposal without demonstrated clinical efficacy**, not advice to carry out eurythmy to treat organ pathology.

### Data-model consequence

Add `metamorphosed_form_of`, `formative_inversion_of`, `anatomical_analogy`, `participates_in_speech_rhythm`, and `historical_therapeutic_claim` as distinct predicates. For larynx, link to posterior head, ear, rib cage, thyroid, rhythm—do not assign invented Old Moon/Sun origin dates.

## 4. Anatomy and moral volition in childhood (GA 317)

In GA 317 §§6–9 Steiner opposes **head synthesis** to **metabolic-limb analysis**. He says that in the head the processes that exist as distinct liver, kidney, stomach or intestinal processes lower in the body are unified, and that this synthesis underlies thinking. The separated inner organs, he says, underlie **willing**. The well-known sentence about a liver being a 'fixed' liver process appears in §7.

In §§10–14 he outlines a hereditary-model theory: before second dentition the child allegedly lives in a parent-given body; the descending individuality rebuilds it during the next age-period, followed by a third organization at puberty. This is **not supported as an account of human genetics or developmental biology**. It must be indexed separately as `historical_developmental_theory`.

In §21 Steiner calls the liver a mediator from **intended action to bodily deed**; §22 gives a speculative example in which a father's philosophical treatment of 'will' allegedly helps account for his son's incapacity to act. This is distinct from the GA 205 claim about future *brain cognition* and the GA 316 claim about inward *nutritional sense*: three separate liver relations.

GA 317 §§15–22 contain claims suggesting childhood disability, illness or behavioral differences arise from karmic choices and organ/soul mismatches. Such claims are unproven and can stigmatize disabled people and families. They should **never** be used to explain an actual person's disability or to substitute for evidence-based care.
