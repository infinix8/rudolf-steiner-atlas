## 1. GA 313: an organ cannot be explained by a fixed list of spiritual constituents

### A. Different body systems show different modes of the same members

In [GA 313 I, §§4–9](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA313/English/SOL/19210411.html), the head, chest and metabolic-limb systems are not given *exclusive* ownership of the four members. Instead, Steiner introduces a distinction between **primary active organization** and **imprint**. In the head, the physical process is primary, with etheric, astral and I activity appearing as *imprints*. In the chest and respiratory rhythms, the physical and etheric work together as primary, while the astral and I are imprints. In the metabolic-limb realm, physical, etheric and astral are mutually active, with the I leaving its imprint.

This gives us a new `relation_type: primary_organization_vs_imprint`. It does **not** say the head lacks an astral or an I, nor that the heart lacks one. In fact, the distinction explains why a general statement about an organ's astral role can coexist with a claim about its etheric function.

### B. Organs are differentiated by processes, not merely substances

In [GA 313 I §§2–3](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA313/English/SOL/19210411.html) Steiner says one must begin from *processes* rather than static substances. He applies silica and calcium process analogies to head formation (§6) and describes a gradient of silica *force* versus silica *material* from head to limbs (§§10–11). Most relevant to the organ atlas, in §17 he describes formative influences descending from the head and **differentiating into particular organs such as the heart and liver**.

This is not a new Old Saturn origin story. It is an ongoing **organ differentiation** account. Steiner repeatedly contrasts matter's presence with matter's asserted activity—silica in hair versus urine, magnesium in teeth versus milk (§19). His particular physiological assertions are unproven and sometimes erroneous, but his methodological distinction is important to understanding his arguments.

### C. Head, etheric world and eye

In [GA 313 II §§2–9](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA313/English/SOL/19210412.html), the I's head imprint is framed through *differentiated warmth*, while its limb imprint is a dynamic configuration of equilibrium, walking and posture. The etheric organization is not an undifferentiated cloud; he distinguishes warmth, light, chemical and life ethers (§§3–5). The eye is described as organized by light (§7). He presents the head as predominantly receptive to warmth and light ethers (§8), whereas the metabolic-limb organization supposedly draws chemical and life ethers upward (§9).

This is a **spatial directional force model**, not evidence that 'eye = Old Sun' or that an angel unique to the eye has been identified.

### D. Chest as interaction, not sealed physiological unit

In [GA 313 III §§2–3](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA313/English/SOL/19210413.html), the chest organs are a physical–etheric interplay influenced by astral and I organization. Their rhythmic condition is presented as an effect of the ongoing polarities of head and metabolism. In §§7–8 he speculates that longstanding emotions and recurring hunger/thirst disturbances can affect rhythmic bodily functions. Those latter causal medical interpretations are historical claims, not diagnosis.

## 2. GA 315: therapeutic claims are not evidence of organ creation

The [second lecture](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA315/English/SOL/19210413.html) develops a model of vowel gestures: an I movement related to walking and circulation (§4), a U movement for standing (§7), and a broader five-vowel spiritual characterization (§18). The [third](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA315/English/SOL/19210414.html) contrasts outward consonantal articulation with inward vocalic articulation (§§1–3), and describes bodily eurythmy as the polar reversal of speech reflected from the larynx (§6).

The [fourth](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA315/English/SOL/19210415.html) applies specific consonant/limb gestures to kidney function (§2), constipation (§3), intestinal peristalsis (§4), urination (§6) and bowel function (§13). These should be indexed as `historical_therapeutic_claim` or `speech_movement_correspondence`, **not** as proof of therapeutic efficacy or as a timetable of anatomical formation.

There are stenographic uncertainties in some GA 315 notes, including the original transcription of words describing particular gestures. Treatment claims require especially careful original-language comparison. Avoid recommending these actions as treatment.

## 3. GA 317: etheric thought and permeability of organ boundaries

### A. Brain as alleged receiver of pre-existing thought

[GA 317 II §§11–15](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA317/English/SOL/19240626.html) continues the head-synthesis argument from GA 317 I. Steiner maintains that living thoughts already exist in a universal ether and that the human etheric organization carries those possibilities into incarnation. In this model, the brain is **not the producer** of thought, but becomes capable of **reflecting** them into ordinary awareness. This is a claim about spiritual cognition and bodily function, not a supported theory of neurobiology.

In §§20–25 he relates an alleged weak etheric organization of the liver to difficulty converting intentions into bodily actions, extending the **liver/will** theme first recorded from GA 317 I. The surrounding discussion sometimes attributes childhood difficulties to karma or educators' spiritual organization; none of those claims is valid medical or educational assessment.

### B. Eye, gravity and waking

[GA 317 III §§6–11](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA317/English/SOL/19240627.html) links the I organization to gravitational, earthly and thermal forces and connects the astral organization with light, chemistry and life ether. In §11 the eye is used as an outward-open sense organ enabling direct encounter with external forces. In §§12–13, Steiner speculates that an 'I' and astral organization trapped by lung or other organ surfaces could explain seizures; this is **medically false** as an explanation of epilepsy and must never guide care.

### C. Organs as boundaries

[GA 317 IV §§5–7](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA317/English/SOL/19240628.html) offers a complementary scheme. Steiner says that an organ's relation to astral and I organization may fail in two opposite directions: alleged **congestion** of supersensible members inside an organ (linked by him with spasm and pain) or excessive **permeability/outflow** (which he relates to secretions). In §16 he compares the latter process to the soul-spiritual expansion he imagines at death.

This is a new `organ_boundary_permeability` family, not the same as the **organ-surface memory** in GA 205, and it does not authenticate Steiner's unsupported medical theories.

## 5. Quality-control flags

- **Source records are selected readings only.** The Phase 8 record count (49) does not mean 49 non-overlapping teachings; previous datasets can contain the same passage or overlapping claims.
- **No German collations completed in this pass.** In GA 315, stenographic variants make fine-grained wording especially fragile; English choices are provisionally recorded.
- **Body-part taxonomy is not an anatomical ontology.** Terms such as 'liver-gall system', 'rhythmic chest', 'head', 'eye' and 'I organization' need separate normalized IDs and type links.
- **Scientific and clinical boundary.** Steiner's assertions about liver causation, epilepsy, medical treatments, child disabilities and reincarnational inheritance are not established by research. Do not present them as therapy or as explanations for living persons.
- **Audit queue after pass:** GA 313–317 has 6 fully numbered-English-reviewed entries, 1 thematic sweep, 9 selected-passage reviews, 45 entirely unreviewed; 0 certified exhaustive original-German atomic concordances. Continue by **full-lecture** extraction of GA 313 I–III and GA 317 II–IV, with transparent zero-hit logs and German collation.
