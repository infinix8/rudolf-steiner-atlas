## Reported observation is not a demonstrated cause

The case lectures move repeatedly between supplied histories, live demonstrations, retrospective anecdotes, conjectures and general assertions. These levels remain separate in the [section audit](../research/organ-paragraph-review-ga317-vi-vii-v15.json) and selected claims. VI §6 hypothesizes a mechanical injury and assigns karmic significance; VII §17 infers an unreported premature birth despite the supplied history. Neither belongs in a factual patient-event register. VI §13's hydrocephalus improvement is a retrospective anecdote, not independent evidence for treatment efficacy.

## Corrections and open comparisons

The [German/textual log](../research/organ-german-checks-and-textual-issues-v15.json) identifies two consequential translation issues. VI §22's German describes pituitary secretion as meeting a force flow and harmonizing head-to-limb relations; SOL's “counteracting” can imply the opposite direction. The record therefore uses the more cautious supporting/harmonizing formulation and leaves edition comparison open. VII §16 has **Balkenstich**, rendered “balancer injection” in SOL. Retain the German term pending an independent edition/technical check; do not index the English phrase as a verified injection.

VII §§12,23 extend the earlier organ-wall/convulsion theory to a particular case and explicitly generalize the obstruction to all organs. This is overlap and changed scope, not discovery of a new organ-specific epilepsy cause. VI §15 and VII §21 add waking/return contexts to the older sleep evidence; they do not settle the interruption versus redistribution tension. VII §18's digestive I account also leaves the earlier weak/excess I diarrhea tension open.

Maternal theater/R-pronunciation causation, karmic disability explanations, demon language, astral seizure/paralysis mechanisms and the minimization of paralysis remain flagged as unsupported, false, stigmatizing or unsafe claims as appropriate. Arsenic bathing and gland extract proposals are historical material, not treatment guidance. Narrative and movement pedagogy must not be used to obscure those claims.
