## Organ interfaces and explanatory scales

The central editorial finding is a distinction among organ formation, present function, spiritual contact and pathological interruption. An organ's alleged role as an interface does not establish its cosmic origin or identify a spiritual builder. Brain, lung, liver, eye and spleen propositions are indexed with their own predicates and temporal frameworks. The generic prenatal gods reference in IV §2 supplies no rank or exclusive organ assignment.

III §6 requires a substantive correction: **ORG-V8-041 reversed direct and indirect contact**. The new record preserves the old ID as a correction target rather than silently rewriting historical evidence. III §5 also contains a translation issue: German describes return upon waking, whereas SOL English begins with withdrawal. The German discussion limits overcoming gravity to consciousness; it must not become an assertion that measured body mass disappears.

IV's retention/outflow polarity is historically indexed, including the secretion and glandular passages. These alleged pathological perceptions are **not** the deliberate, disciplined spiritual development described in other Steiner works. Nor does a reference to an organ perceiving excess astrality establish a clinical mechanism, a named hierarchy, or an acquired creative power.

## Corrections and unresolved tensions

- ORG-V9-018 points to ORG-V8-044, but its appropriate earlier comparison is **ORG-V8-042**. ORG-V9-019 points to ORG-V8-046, but should compare **ORG-V8-044**. ORG-V9-017 points to ORG-V8-036, but the liver/intention record is **ORG-V8-040**. Original files are preserved.
- GA 317 III §3 describes interrupted astral/I connection during sleep; GA 313 II §26 and III §5 describe regional redistribution/continued lower activity. This is an **apparent conflict of explanatory scope**, not a resolved equivalence. A full German comparison of all three passages remains necessary.
- IV §4's SOL formula and German transcription typography are uncertain. No correction to Steiner's underlying chemistry is inferred from malformed digital formulas without a printed-edition check.
- IV §10's English phrasing obscures the German contrast between too weak and too strong environmental contact. The original terms are logged; no entire-lecture bilingual status is claimed.

The false neurological, appendicitis and phrenological claims, moralized disability descriptions, auric diagnostic assertions and historical treatment proposals are explicitly attributed and flagged. Their presence is evidence about Steiner's text, not validation or practical guidance. The same applies to deliberate anxiety/shock procedures: historical indexing is not endorsement.
