## Development and consciousness

[Lecture I §§8–10](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/Compare_01.php) describes brain formation as spinal-cord metamorphosis and calls its formative history older than the present cord. German confirms the counterintuitive age wording. It does not say a living person’s spinal tissue is younger than their brain tissue; nor does it predict that today’s cord becomes another enlarged brain. The dream-organ account is initially hypothetical and occult, not identification of a second anatomical cord.

[Lecture II §11](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/Compare_02.php) initially introduces Saturn–spleen, Jupiter–liver and Mars–Galle as provisional names for internalized worlds. Galle requires contextual care because it can denote bile as well as biliary organ activity. Planetary nomenclature alone does not establish an organ’s evolutionary origin or a named spiritual hierarchy builder.

[Lecture III §§11–18](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/English/SOL/19110322p01.html) develops the Saturnian spleen as rhythmic independence from irregular intake. It also introduces a qualification: self-contained rhythm must eventually be reconciled with a wider order. The Cronus story is interpreted symbolically. It is not a literal assertion of an organ-eating being. The account expressly allows more splenic functions than this example.

## Spleen qualifications and comparison

[Lecture IV §§3–6](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA128/Compare_04.php) directly answers a splenectomy objection by distinguishing physical organ and supersensible forces. Steiner claims the forces persist after removal; this is an attributed metaphysical assertion, not independently demonstrated compensation. It cannot be used to validate the later rabbit tissue observation in GA316-09 §15 as physical regeneration.

The next paragraph calls correction of meal timing the easiest but least significant splenic function. The wider task is adaptation of food’s purported inherent laws to human organization. This qualification prevents the atlas from reducing the entire spleen dossier to regular meal schedules.

## Heart kidneys lungs and memory

Lecture IV §§8–13 locates the heart at the meeting of respiratory exposure and digestive internalization, assigns kidneys harmonization and excess elimination, and names heart Sun, lungs Mercury and kidneys Venus. These functional correspondences differ from Old Sun/Old Moon meditative stage references in GA316. Their relationship remains an explicit comparison question.

At §§19–21, blood-associated currents ascend from heart toward head, while an opposing current is associated especially with lymphatic vessels. The pineal gland expresses the memory-forming side; the pituitary expresses the receiving side. German confirms both assignments. Etheric current condensation is a spiritual organogenesis claim, not established glandular function or memory physiology.
