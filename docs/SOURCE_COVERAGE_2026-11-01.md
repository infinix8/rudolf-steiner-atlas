## Quality principle

Do not inflate coverage by adding a lecture-series index as a verified passage. Every individual source record must identify the actual authored chapter, dated lecture, or dated essay, edition where known, and a link that has been opened. Every doctrinal relation requires its own substantive audit before marking reviewed. Archived site content is not copied.
