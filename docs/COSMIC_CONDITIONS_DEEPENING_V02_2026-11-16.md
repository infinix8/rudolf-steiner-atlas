## Reading instructions and scholarly caveat

These are Rudolf Steiner's spiritual-historical and cosmological claims, **not verified astronomical, geological, anatomical or medical history**. Source pointers use an archive link to the relevant chapter or lecture together with a printed **[§] paragraph cue** where the source is divided by numbered paragraphs. We have checked pertinent English passages in GA 13, GA 11 and GA 104, but have **not** performed comprehensive German-language comparison, lecture-cycle extraction, independent second-source audit, or modern scientific validation. The author-revised GA 13 book, GA 11 articles and recorded GA 104 lecture have different textual statuses. This research does **not** turn spiritual beings into empirically measurable causal explanations for illness or organ function. Attribution and caution flags must follow the nodes into any visualization.

The word **'physical'** in Old Saturn does *not* mean solid human flesh; **Old Sun** and **Old Moon** are past conditions and **Future Jupiter/Venus/Vulcan** are prospective cosmic states, not familiar celestial bodies. The description of an agent as prominent in one stage **does not mean the old hierarchy disappears** when the next rank acts. Likewise, a first *germ* of an organ is not a completed modern organ.

## Seven stage dossiers

### 1. Old Saturn
**Hierarchy path:** `cos-saturn` · **Position:** 1 of 7 — first planetary embodiment · **Status:** past.
**Summary.** An original warmth condition in Steiner's account, establishing the first physical-body germ while the hierarchies themselves undergo development. It is neither the astronomical planet Saturn nor a modern material body.
**Consciousness.** Dim, trance-like consciousness, compared to the consciousness of minerals; not a waking human self.
**Human constitution.** Physical-body germ, without the separately incorporated human etheric, astral or I organizations. A Spirit-Man germ is mentioned, but not a completed higher human faculty.
**Element / physicality.** Warmth rather than current gas, water or solid mineral matter; Steiner separates a 'physical body' governed by physical laws from a contemporary mineralized body.
**Agencies and relationships**

- Spirits of Will / Thrones: primal outpouring and later shaping
- Spirits of Wisdom: reflected life, not a fully independent human life-body
- Spirits of Movement and Spirits of Form: reflected feeling and differentiated forms
- Spirits of Personality / Archai: their 'human' phase
- Fire Spirits / Archangels and Seraphim: first sense-organ germs and assisting consciousness

**Passage-checked findings**

1. **The physical germ is not current flesh.** Steiner distinguishes the warmth-based physical body from the mineralized human organism of the Earth.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §§16, 20–21 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
2. **Higher beings work together.** Reflections of life, feeling and outward personality appear through several cooperating ranks; the activities are not exclusive to one epoch.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §§21–22, 29 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
3. **The first sensory germs.** The Fire Spirits are associated with first sense-organ archetypes, in conjunction with Seraphim; these are not fully formed organs.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §23 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
4. **Time becomes manifest.** In Steiner's distinction, 'Duration' precedes manifest Time; language of earlier-before is analogical.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §27 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
5. **Germ of Spirit-Man.** A very early Spirit-Man seed is connected to Saturn, not the achieved future transformation by the I.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §25 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
6. **Preservation through world-rest.** The Saturn seed is dissolved as manifestation yet carried forward and reworked after cosmic rest.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §§29–30 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)

**Editorial cautions**

- Do not identify Old Saturn with the current astronomical body.
- A 'physical' germ does not mean tissue, chemistry or a fully formed skeleton.
- Hierarchy roles overlap; avoid a single exclusive creator per organ.

### 2. Old Sun
**Hierarchy path:** `cos-sun` · **Position:** 2 of 7 — second planetary embodiment · **Status:** past.
**Summary.** The previous physical germ is recapitulated and permeated by an etheric or life-body; warmth condenses toward a gas-like element, and the outward conditions of light and plant-like activity appear.
**Consciousness.** Consciousness analogous to dreamless sleep or the contemporary plant state, not ordinary self-consciousness.
**Human constitution.** Physical body advances to a second stage; the etheric life-body first becomes an enduring independent principle. Later germinal sensory and glandular differentiation is described.
**Element / physicality.** Warmth and air/gas, with the emergence of light; not the physical solar body familiar from astronomy.
**Agencies and relationships**

- Spirits of Wisdom / Kyriotetes: pouring forth the life-body
- Spirits of Movement: motions within the living organization
- Spirits of Form: stabilization of forms
- Spirits of Personality and Seraphim: etheric activity and beginnings of reproduction
- Fire Spirits / Archangels: work on inherited Saturn basis and sensory germs
- Sons of Life / Angels and Cherubim: later life processes

**Passage-checked findings**

1. **Not a fresh creation from nothing.** Sun begins with a transformed repetition of Old Saturn and continues its acquired potential.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §§30–31 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
2. **A differentiated life-body.** Wisdom beings endow the germ with etheric life; physical and life-body are described as differentiating while remaining interpenetrated.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §§31–32 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
3. **From warmth toward gas.** Sun is described through air/gaseous structures and plant-like flowing rather than today's plants.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §§32–33 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
4. **Different roles in sense and glandular origins.** The Archangels affect sense-organ precursors; the Spirits of Personality in union with Seraphim are linked to glandular beginnings.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §§35–36 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
5. **A new Saturn-like sphere.** Steiner relates lagging Personality spirits to a separated secondary heavenly body; this is a stage-specific esoteric account.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §35 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
6. **World-rest follows Sun.** The results are carried through a rest before the Old Moon metamorphosis.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §§38–39 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)

**Editorial cautions**

- Do not treat etheric as a confirmed scientific or medical substance.
- The source refers to precursors of glandular organs rather than present-day glands already functioning.

### 3. Old Moon
**Hierarchy path:** `cos-moon` · **Position:** 3 of 7 — third planetary embodiment · **Status:** past.
**Summary.** The already formed physical and etheric organizations undergo a further metamorphosis through the incorporation of the astral body, giving the human ancestor feeling and picture consciousness.
**Consciousness.** Dreamlike imaginative/picture consciousness, associated with the environment rather than waking objective cognition.
**Human constitution.** Astral organization is added; sentient and intellectual soul beginnings are described as seeds rather than complete terrestrial faculties.
**Element / physicality.** Warmth and gas plus a liquid/water-like element; not yet the contemporary solid mineral form.
**Agencies and relationships**

- Spirits of Movement: astral incorporation and sensing
- Spirits of Form: emerging wish, craving and stable tendencies
- Spiritual Sun beings: influence on the separated Moon sphere
- Spirits of Wisdom: later wisdom-giving work
- Sons of Life / Angels: their 'human' stage within the Moon account

**Passage-checked findings**

1. **Repetition precedes the new stage.** Old Moon restages Saturn and Sun conditions in altered form before the astral principle is incorporated.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §39 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
2. **Astral body and impulse.** Movement produces sentient soul qualities; Form works upon unstable sympathy and antipathy to give rise to primitive desire.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §§39–40 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
3. **Condensation into a watery condition.** Steiner characterizes the body's three conditions as water-like, gaseous and warmth forms.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §40 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
4. **The Sun–Moon relationship.** Separation permits differentiated spiritual influence, later alternating relationships and a concluding reunion.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §§41–50, 55–56 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
5. **Picture awareness.** The Moon's dream-images correspond in Steiner's description to supersensible surroundings, unlike an individual's ordinary modern dream fragments.
   - Source: GA 104 lecture 10 §§7–8 — [S12: GA 104, lecture 10, 27 June 1908.](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080627p01.html)
6. **Sowing seeds of human soul-members.** The late Old Moon description connects the Sons of Life to the beginnings of sentient and intellectual soul; the free terrestrial I is still future.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §§57–58 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)

**Editorial cautions**

- Old Moon must not be conflated with Earth's current satellite.
- The source's plant-mineral/animal-man terms are analogical esoteric classifications, not prehistoric species descriptions.

### 4. Earth
**Hierarchy path:** `cos-earth` · **Position:** 4 of 7 — present planetary embodiment · **Status:** present.
**Summary.** The previous physical, etheric and astral germinal achievements are recapitulated; the independent human I enters a newly mineralized and sensory world, giving rise to objective awareness and a new moral task.
**Consciousness.** Object consciousness and independent self-consciousness: external spatial objects and reflective thinking become salient.
**Human constitution.** Physical body in a fourth condition of maturation, etheric in third, astral in second, and I at its beginning; later conscious transformation of these members is the task.
**Element / physicality.** The fully developed solid/mineral condition emerges alongside inherited warmth, air and water.
**Agencies and relationships**

- Spirits of Form / Exusiai: Earth-related conditions of the I in the broader Steiner corpus
- Christ impulse: the transformation of inherited wisdom toward love
- Earlier hierarchies: not replaced; their work remains embedded in the human members

**Passage-checked findings**

1. **The I enters three earlier bodies.** Steiner explains the transition to Earth by locating an earlier physical, life and astral constitution before the human I began to work within it.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §§3–6 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
2. **Different ages of the four members.** The physical germ is called oldest; the I is the youngest, rather than the only spiritually significant member.
   - Source: GA 13 IV §§16–17 — [S01: GA 13, Occult Science — An Outline, chapter IV, "The Evolution of the Cosmos and Man."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
3. **Outer objects and inward thought.** The terrestrial waking condition differentiates the outward object world from inward concepts, memory and the sense of self.
   - Source: GA 11 chapter XIII §§1–2 — [S10: GA 11, chapter XIII, "The Earth and Its Future."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c13.html)
4. **Earth's wisdom becomes love.** The inherited wisdom of Saturn, Sun and Moon is to be taken inward through the I and transformed into love.
   - Source: GA 13 VI concluding discussion of the Cosmos of Wisdom and Cosmos of Love — [S02: GA 13, chapter VI, "Present and Future Evolution of the World and of Mankind."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c06.html)
5. **Continuity with future Jupiter.** Earth's acquisitions are carried through a later cosmic interval and participate in the next condition rather than being discarded.
   - Source: GA 13 VI §§1, 8 — [S02: GA 13, chapter VI, "Present and Future Evolution of the World and of Mankind."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c06.html)
6. **Distinct Earth subepochs.** Polar, Hyperborean, Lemurian, Atlantean, post-Atlantean and two projected future great epochs belong inside Earth; they are not separate cosmic embodiments.
   - Source: GA 104 lecture 10 §§12 onward — [S12: GA 104, lecture 10, 27 June 1908.](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080627p01.html)

**Editorial cautions**

- The claim that astral disturbances cause illness is Steiner's speculation, not a medical explanation.
- Do not equate development of the I with ordinary personal egotism.
- Future cultural periods and Earth-wide cosmic stages must remain distinct levels.

### 5. Future Jupiter
**Hierarchy path:** `cos-jupiter` · **Position:** 5 of 7 — projected planetary embodiment · **Status:** future.
**Summary.** Steiner projects a later planetary condition arising through the metamorphosis of Earth after world-rest, integrating its new achievements with what was prepared in Old Moon.
**Consciousness.** Self-conscious imaginative or image consciousness: the independent I persists while perceptive images of supersensible reality become accessible.
**Human constitution.** Human spiritual capacities are described as transformed; contemporary mineral forms cease as such, with plant-like forces the lowest kingdom in his account.
**Element / physicality.** Not the current astronomical Jupiter; the account explicitly rejects a direct projection of today's physical material into that condition.
**Agencies and relationships**

- Spiritual inheritance of Old Moon and Earth conditions; no exclusive one-to-one creator claimed here
- Human freedom, moral evolution and future assistance to other beings

**Passage-checked findings**

1. **Earth and Old Moon inheritances unite.** Jupiter is described as future form shaped both by Old Moon preparation and by what human beings accomplish on Earth.
   - Source: GA 13 VI §1 — [S02: GA 13, chapter VI, "Present and Future Evolution of the World and of Mankind."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c06.html)
2. **Self-conscious image-awareness.** Steiner distinguishes Jupiter's controlled images from the older Moon's involuntary dream pictures.
   - Source: GA 11 chapter XIII §§4–5 — [S10: GA 11, chapter XIII, "The Earth and Its Future."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c13.html)
   - Source: GA 104 lecture 10 §9 — [S12: GA 104, lecture 10, 27 June 1908.](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080627p01.html)
3. **Recapitulation before the new stage.** GA 11 says the prior conditions are repeated before specifically Jupiter consciousness becomes established.
   - Source: GA 11 chapter XIII §7 — [S10: GA 11, chapter XIII, "The Earth and Its Future."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c13.html)
4. **Transformation of kingdoms.** Steiner predicts the end of a separately existing mineral kingdom, with a different plant-like kingdom lowest.
   - Source: GA 13 VI §8 — [S02: GA 13, chapter VI, "Present and Future Evolution of the World and of Mankind."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c06.html)
5. **Spiritual responsibility.** The account explicitly makes the development and help of other, less advanced humanity part of the new condition.
   - Source: GA 13 VI §8 — [S02: GA 13, chapter VI, "Present and Future Evolution of the World and of Mankind."](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c06.html)

**Editorial cautions**

- No numerical date or measured duration is given for Future Jupiter.
- The source's moral grouping is not valid for judging contemporary communities or individuals.

## Relationships to preserve in the wheel

**Physical → etheric → astral → I is a cumulative development.** GA 13 chapter IV §§16–17 explicitly distinguishes these ages of the four members. The physical body is oldest and in some sense most formed; the I is youngest, and through it Steiner places the future transmutation of older members. Do not draw a false replacement sequence.

**The hierarchies cooperate.** Steiner explicitly cautions against treating the sequence of spirit agencies as mutually exclusive. A particular rank's prominent action occurs amid ongoing activities of other ranks; this is directly stated in [GA 13 IV §29](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html). When a later organ is traced to another hierarchical agency, record the action type and source rather than assigning a single 'creator' label.

**Saturn, Sun and Moon recapitulations are not the same as the seven Earth epochs.** The earliest cosmic conditions have their own nested conditions and recapitulations; the chronological wheel should not confuse Old Moon with Earth's present Moon. See [GA 11 chapter XIII §§1, 7, 9](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c13.html).

**Earth's distinctive direction: wisdom to love.** GA 13's closing discussion in chapter VI relates the human I's inner transformation to the future "Cosmos of Love"; this is not a mechanical process or a claim that ancient hierarchies cease working. The lecture and book descriptions also connect to the Christ event. Read [GA 13 VI §§10–12](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c06.html).

**Future consciousness is not the recovery of ancient passive clairvoyance.** Old Moon's involuntary dream images differ from the self-conscious image awareness assigned to Future Jupiter. Venus and Vulcan go further toward Inspiration and Intuition; see [GA 104 Tenth Lecture §§9–11](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080627p01.html).

## Open questions and future expansion priorities

1. **Precision of agency.** GA 13 describes main rank assignments (e.g. Spirits of Wisdom and the etheric germ) but other lecture cycles can describe additional roles. Record *what* each hierarchy is doing, not only a fixed agent-to-stage mapping. Reconcile existing GA 102/110 organ-atlas tensions before proclaiming exclusivity.
2. **First germs vs modern organs.** Integrate sensory, glandular, blood, heart, lung, kidney and other organ evidence from the existing audited Organ Atlas; retain the difference between early seeds, modifications, present spiritual function and future tasks.
3. **GA 11 vs GA 104 terminology.** Cross-compare Venus as 'self-conscious object/creative' (GA 11) and as 'inspired' (GA 104); similarly contrast intuitive consciousness and the limitation on descriptions of Vulcan. Check editions and German originals.
4. **Minor conditions (life/form).** Expand only where individual subdivisions and source passages are actually identified. Do not claim that every possible subdivision in sevenfold schemes is explicitly narrated.
5. **Second review and uncertainty.** Conduct comprehensive GA 11 and GA 13 source audits, then independently compare GA 104, 105, 110, 112, 121, 132 and 346 and flag context shifts.
6. **Historical validity and harmful classifications.** Present obsolete race, gender, disease and disability claims as critically attributed source history, never as established anthropology or medical guidance.
