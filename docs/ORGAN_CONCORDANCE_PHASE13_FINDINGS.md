## Findings that change the Atlas structure

### One I, differentiated instruments

GA 313 IV §9 explicitly qualifies upper/lower I language as instruments of one I. This supports a graph with regional activities, rather than separate selves inhabiting different organs. Its neighboring account links nutrition, sensory experience and bodily warmth. These present-life relations remain distinct from the earliest cosmic formation of physical organs.

### Metamorphosis has more than one meaning

GA 313 VI separates a serial organ metamorphosis from a polar one. The distinction affects how the dossiers should be read:

| Relation being indexed | Principal organs | Source | Editorial boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Serial formative metamorphosis | Stomach, liver, lung, head/brain | VI §§4–6 | Not a literal anatomical conversion or verified evolutionary genealogy |
| Polar metamorphosis | Heart and lung | VI §14 | Not the same edge as serial transformation |
| Further transformed organ form | Uterus and heart | VI §14 | Not a claim that adult heart tissue becomes uterus |
| Etheric counterpart | Alleged male etheric uterus | VI §14 | Metaphysical assertion, not anatomical organ |
| Tissue-fluid formation/function | Heart | VI §§11–12 | Not Old Saturn chronology or named hierarchy agency |

The word **Urorgan** in the heart passage should not silently be converted into a dated cosmic origin. The surrounding discussion concerns formation through tissue fluid. Likewise, the liver's alleged inward spiritual activity (VI §§8–10) is a further relation type; it does not replace its earlier planetary, nutritive, will or future-life contexts.

### Bodily processes and spiritual activity

GA 313 V distinguishes arsenic-like force activity from the actual presence of arsenic (§2), before moving to distinct historical remedy proposals. A process analogy therefore needs its own predicate. Its phosphorus/blood account (§§15–17) adds a claimed I–blood-cell boundary, a striated-muscle association, and a tissue/bone degeneration hypothesis. No spiritual hierarchy is named as their exclusive builder.

The poisoning/death-retrospect comparison in V §18 and GA 317 IV's dying/outflow comparison belong to a comparative analogy layer. They are not interchangeable mechanisms or medical predictions. GA 313 VI's organ-imagination hypothesis (§21) likewise concerns purported involuntary pathology; it must not be presented as a safe method for deliberate supersensible development.

### Memory absorption is not organ-surface congestion

GA 317 V §12 expressly distinguishes excessive absorption of impressions from the organ-surface congestion of previous lectures. This prevents merging the two merely because both use inward/outward vocabulary. Its surrounding account places head and metabolic-limb organization in opposed constitutional arrangements, mediated by rhythm. These are schematic relations, not spatial shells established by anatomy. A diagram/deictic ambiguity around §5 remains unresolved; the explicit descriptions in §§5–7 are indexed without repairing the transcript by conjecture.

Hair-color/sulfur/iron classification, behavioral predictions about disability or mania, dietary treatments and verse-induced protein changes are recorded as unsupported historical claims. Their specificity must not be mistaken for diagnostic validity. GA 317 V §26 also contains an unnumbered third-person editorial account; the section audit includes it as context without attributing its exact words as Steiner's verbatim speech.
