The [v0.1 master chronology](STEINER_HIERARCHICAL_CHRONOLOGY_MASTER_V01.md) and the [v0.2 cosmic dossiers](COSMIC_CONDITIONS_DEEPENING_V02_2026-11-16.md) supply the cumulative backbone. This concordance adds targeted primary-text audits within that sequence. The [structured audit](../research/steiner-earth-culture-audit-v03.json) records sources, graph edges, organ references and coverage. The [continuation checkpoint](CHRONOLOGY_RESEARCH_RESUME.md) tracks unfinished reading.

Read these accounts as Steiner’s esoteric interpretations and forecasts. They do not establish geological or cultural history, anatomical development or medical knowledge. Early sources contain obsolete racial classifications and gender stereotypes. The source terms identify the textual typology only.

## Earth epochs and cultural periods

### Etheric emergence and earliest responsiveness (`earth-polar`)

GA 11 VIII changes the reference point from preterrestrial astral life to etheric embodiment. Its hearing analogy describes a whole-body function.

- **EP01 · Etheric embodiment and hearing before the ear.** Steiner places dreamlike astral image consciousness before entry into etheric materiality. External etheric vibration then yields an organism-wide precursor of hearing before a separate organ exists. [GA 11, VIII. The Hyperborean and the Polarean Epoch, §§ 2–3](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c08.html)

### Differentiation of body and environment (`earth-hyper`)

GA 11 VIII groups the first two epochs in one retrospective account. Its gradual cosmic separation in §10 should be read alongside the more detailed order in IX–X.

- **EP02 · Reproduction, death and perceptual differentiation.** With condensation, reproduction changes from division to a locally mediated process, and the remaining body may disintegrate. Hearing localizes while touch still belongs to most of the body. Section 6 names the two early stages after the description of reproduction and death. [GA 11, VIII. The Hyperborean and the Polarean Epoch, §§ 4–7](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c08.html)
- **EP03 · External sunlight and the first eye rudiments.** Part of the light ether is withdrawn at solar separation and subsequently acts from outside. Steiner relates this altered dependence to the first organs receptive to sunlight, followed by further densification and the fire-mist human constitution. The chapter itself gives relative order, while Hyperborean placement is an editorial crosswalk. [GA 11, IX. Beginning of the Present Earth—Extrusion of the Sun, §§ 7–9](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c09.html)

### Embodiment, differentiation and new inward powers (`earth-lemur`)

GA 11 supplies descriptions of pre-division anatomy, local group development and a volcanic transition. GA 13 relates form, I, lunar withdrawal and agency without supplying a calendar.

- **EP04 · Reproductive force redirected to inward organs.** Steiner pictures older and newer reproductive forms coexisting. He connects density with redirecting part of the prior reproductive capacity into inward organs, mentioning the brain as an instrument of inwardly acquired cognition. He distinguishes early incomplete formation from readiness for spirit contact around mid-Lemuria. [GA 11, VI. The Division into Sexes, §§ 2–5, 8–9](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c06.html)
- **EP05 · Mobility and senses before division into sexes.** In the late pre-division account, movement organs are comparatively developed. Hearing and touch are present earlier than light perception, which develops later in the life described. The text also situates extra-parental maturation and externally supplied heat before the later reproductive organization. [GA 11, VII. The Last Periods before the Division into Sexes, §§ 1–6](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c07.html)
- **EP06 · Two forms of spiritual influence on early humanity.** Steiner assigns regulation of reproduction to advanced beings acting on the majority as instinct. A separate, less advanced group directs inner images toward the sensory environment, interrupting what he describes as an otherwise dreamlike spiritual life. [GA 11, VII. The Last Periods before the Division into Sexes, §§ 7–10](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c07.html)
- **EP07 · Fire mist, upright form and divided guidance.** Thinking organs are prepared in the fire mist. After lunar separation and cooling, Steiner links upstanding posture and hand rudiments with altered functions of the upper body. He assigns lower-body guidance to Moon beings and renewed influence over developed personality to Luciferic beings. [GA 11, X. Extrusion of the Moon, §§ 3–6](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c10.html)
- **EP08 · Moon separation, form and generation.** GA 13 describes the withdrawal of hardening forces as making individual embodiment possible. It relates emerging sexual differentiation to the distribution of seed and quickening capacities and describes lunar forces continuing from outside Earth. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 69–71, 78](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- **EP09 · Breath, warmth and early bodily systems.** GA 13 attributes guidance of breath into the body to Sun beings. It distinguishes warmth currents as beginnings of blood circulation from an internal differentiation of air as a basis of the later nervous system. These are claimed antecedents, with no implication of modern anatomical completion. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 71–73](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- **EP10 · Interrupted embodiment, I and return.** Some souls can no longer inhabit hardening forms and continue elsewhere. Spirits of Form kindle the I, whose experience carries into body-free existence. As suitable forms become available after Moon separation, formerly displaced souls return over a long interval. The account distinguishes these first returns from repeat Earth incarnations. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 74, 77–78, 87](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- **EP11 · Lunar organization and two adverse influences.** The Moon beings shape the relation of I and bodies. Luciferic intervention grants greater control of consciousness images alongside error and dependence on astral impulses. Steiner separately introduces Ahrimanic influence through earthly forces and fear before its stronger mid-Atlantean manifestation. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 80–82, 88](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- **EP12 · Different nighttime agencies act concurrently.** With alternating day and night, Steiner differentiates the nighttime sphere of activity around I and astral body from the simultaneous activity around physical and life bodies. Spirits of Form are common to both lists. This describes an early Earth rhythm, with a specific allocation of agencies rather than exclusive builders. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 85–87](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- **EP13 · Will, memory, speech and differentiated guidance.** GA 11 describes will-directed schooling and temple practice, and connects late Lemurian memory and elementary moral concepts to an account of women's role and of rhythmic utterance. The source uses gender essentialism and evolutionary ranking. Its local continuity claim reaches into early Atlantis while the fourth subgroup is in preparation. [GA 11, V. The Lemurian Race, §§ 2, 6–13, 18](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c05.html)
- **EP14 · Volcanic destruction and a quieter precursor settlement.** GA 11 depicts volcanism destroying Lemurian territory while a relatively quiet area supports settlement and formation of an Atlantean precursor group. The account distinguishes the destructive environment from development in the refuge. [GA 11, V. The Lemurian Race, §§ 14–16](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c05.html)

### Memory, organization, independent thinking and transition (`earth-atlant`)

Read the seven overlapping phases in GA 11 III alongside the broad account of altered life-body, oracle and sensory experience in GA 13 IV. The sources provide no exact alignment for every process.

- **EP15 · Overlapping phases and memory-led naming.** Subgroups in Steiner's early taxonomy persist alongside successors. The first named subgroup emphasizes retained sense impressions, attachment to past experience and the development of naming already incipient in late Lemuria. [GA 11, III. Our Atlantean Ancestors, §§ 7–11](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c03.html)
- **EP16 · Second phase and shared memory of deeds.** Steiner describes remembered personal achievement as a new basis of leadership and ancestor veneration. Recalled joint undertakings could bind groups in addition to ties of descent. [GA 11, III. Our Atlantean Ancestors, §§ 12–13](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c03.html)
- **EP17 · Third phase, inherited authority and experimental settlement.** The third phase consolidates communal institutions, transmits leadership across generations, and uses remembered experience and experiments when establishing communities. Steiner sees an opening for personal appropriation of nature powers in the same process. [GA 11, III. Our Atlantean Ancestors, §§ 14–16](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c03.html)
- **EP18 · Fourth and fifth phases change the role of power.** Steiner places intensified egoistic use of life forces in the fourth phase and the use of comparison and judgment to regulate desire in the fifth. He describes a tradeoff between inward combinatory thought and immediate command of living forces. [GA 11, III. Our Atlantean Ancestors, §§ 17–19](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c03.html)
- **EP19 · Sixth and seventh phases differ in uses of thinking.** The sixth phase applies thinking more broadly to social order and law. The seventh is depicted as retaining greater attachment to inherited memory and belief in life powers while also developing thought. The source's extension of this typology to living populations is unsupported racial stereotyping. [GA 11, III. Our Atlantean Ancestors, §§ 20–22](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c03.html)
- **EP20 · Pictorial memory, education and seed-force technology.** GA 11 presents imitation and remembered cases as a basis of judgment and education, with initiates treated separately. It claims growth forces in seeds could be redirected to devices under different environmental conditions. These are esoteric technological claims without archaeological or physical verification. [GA 11, III. Our Atlantean Ancestors, §§ 1–5](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c03.html)
- **EP21 · Planetary affinities, sleep and distinct oracle practices.** GA 13 places the fuller differentiation of planetary affiliations in Atlantis. It distinguishes what sleepers and different kinds of initiates could experience through portions of the life-body, and calls the Christ oracle a prophetic stream within this wider field. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 91–94](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- **EP22 · Bodily stabilization and the mid-Atlantean crisis.** GA 13 connects bodily stabilization with variable plasticity, while naming misuse of growth and reproductive powers, including by some initiates, as a cause of destructive air and water changes. It separately emphasizes Ahrimanic distortion of sensory and postmortem experience from mid-Atlantis. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 95–97](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- **EP23 · Oracle preservation and the brain as instrument of thought.** The Sun oracle is credited with keeping and passing on etheric patterns of initiates. Among a successor group Steiner describes closer physical–etheric union, diminished older memory and the transformation of the brain into a needed instrument of thought and bodily self-awareness. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 98–99](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- **EP24 · Manu, independent understanding and a new type of initiate.** GA 11 describes selection of an eastward successor group, spiritual ideas addressed to its own thinking, and training of earthly thinkers as initiates. Later communities take on different forms as that teaching blends with older local inheritances. [GA 11, IV. Transition of the Fourth into the Fifth Root Race, §§ 4–8, 10–12](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c04.html)
- **EP25 · Two catastrophe narratives have distinct claimed mechanisms.** The Lemurian transition is connected to I-related use of fire under Luciferic influence. The Atlantean transition is linked to misuse of growth and generation under different conditions, with air and water effects and further migration. Both accounts are Steiner's esoteric prehistory. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 90, 96–99](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)

## Source comparisons

- **Timing and mechanism of reproductive differentiation** (open). GA 11 VI §§2–5 foregrounds densification and redirected soul force. GA 13 IV §§69–70 ties the beginning of structural differentiation to changed forces at Moon departure. An overlapping process is plausible. Exact equivalence of the causal sequences and their wording awaits German comparison. Evidence EP04, EP08.
- **Two placements for Ahrimanic influence** (qualified distinction). GA 13 IV §88 already connects Ahrimanic beings to fear in early Earth embodiment. IV §97 specifies a further mid-Atlantean distortion of sensory and postmortem experience. Treat the latter as a specific intensification, with the source naming both effects. Evidence EP11, EP22.
- **Brain and thinking organs have several developmental thresholds** (qualified distinction). GA 11 VI §§8–9 distinguishes incomplete organ formation and readiness for spirit contact. GA 11 X §§3–6 links preparations to the fire mist and later use by Luciferic entities. GA 13 IV §99 links the Atlantean brain as an instrument of thought to physical–etheric union. The texts use different developmental predicates. Exact anatomical equivalence remains open. Evidence EP04, EP07, EP23.
- **Early sight rudiments versus developed light perception** (open). GA 11 IX §7 puts first ocular rudiments after Sun separation. VII §3 characterizes light perception as comparatively late in the late pre-division account and later in individual development. Separate an organ rudiment, a function and its timing in a life before attempting an exact crosswalk. Evidence EP03, EP05.
- **Named Atlantean phase and approximate midpoint** (open). GA 11 III §§16–19 assigns particular changes in misuse and judgment to successive named phases. GA 13 IV §§96–97 places a spreading crisis around mid-Atlantis without naming a subphase. The passages do not themselves identify the fourth GA 11 phase with the midpoint of GA 13. Evidence EP18, EP22.
