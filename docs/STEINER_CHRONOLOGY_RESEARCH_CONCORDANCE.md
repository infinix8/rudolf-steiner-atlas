Read these accounts as Steiner’s esoteric interpretations and forecasts. They do not establish geological or cultural history, anatomical development or medical knowledge. ## Earth epochs and cultural periods

### Etheric emergence and earliest responsiveness (`earth-polar`)

GA 11 VIII changes the reference point from preterrestrial astral life to etheric embodiment. Its hearing analogy describes a whole-body function.

- <a id='finding-ep01'></a> **EP01 · Etheric embodiment and hearing before the ear.** Steiner places dreamlike astral image consciousness before entry into etheric materiality. External etheric vibration then yields an organism-wide precursor of hearing before a separate organ exists. [GA 11, VIII. The Hyperborean and the Polarean Epoch, §§ 2–3](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c08.html)

### Differentiation of body and environment (`earth-hyper`)

GA 11 VIII groups the first two epochs in one retrospective account. Its gradual cosmic separation in §10 should be read alongside the more detailed order in IX–X.

- <a id='finding-ep02'></a> **EP02 · Reproduction, death and perceptual differentiation.** With condensation, reproduction changes from division to a locally mediated process, and the remaining body may disintegrate. Hearing localizes while touch still belongs to most of the body. Section 6 names the two early stages after the description of reproduction and death. [GA 11, VIII. The Hyperborean and the Polarean Epoch, §§ 4–7](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c08.html)
- <a id='finding-ep03'></a> **EP03 · External sunlight and the first eye rudiments.** Part of the light ether is withdrawn at solar separation and subsequently acts from outside. Steiner relates this altered dependence to the first organs receptive to sunlight, followed by further densification and the fire-mist human constitution. The chapter itself gives relative order, while Hyperborean placement is an editorial crosswalk. [GA 11, IX. Beginning of the Present Earth—Extrusion of the Sun, §§ 7–9](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c09.html)

### Embodiment, differentiation and new inward powers (`earth-lemur`)

GA 11 supplies descriptions of pre-division anatomy, local group development and a volcanic transition. GA 13 relates form, I, lunar withdrawal and agency without supplying a calendar.

- <a id='finding-ep04'></a> **EP04 · Reproductive force redirected to inward organs.** Steiner pictures older and newer reproductive forms coexisting. He connects density with redirecting part of the prior reproductive capacity into inward organs, mentioning the brain as an instrument of inwardly acquired cognition. He distinguishes early incomplete formation from readiness for spirit contact around mid-Lemuria. [GA 11, VI. The Division into Sexes, §§ 2–5, 8–9](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c06.html)
- <a id='finding-ep05'></a> **EP05 · Mobility and senses before division into sexes.** In the late pre-division account, movement organs are comparatively developed. Hearing and touch are present earlier than light perception, which develops later in the life described. The text also situates extra-parental maturation and externally supplied heat before the later reproductive organization. [GA 11, VII. The Last Periods before the Division into Sexes, §§ 1–6](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c07.html)
- <a id='finding-ep06'></a> **EP06 · Two forms of spiritual influence on early humanity.** Steiner assigns regulation of reproduction to advanced beings acting on the majority as instinct. A separate, less advanced group directs inner images toward the sensory environment, interrupting what he describes as an otherwise dreamlike spiritual life. [GA 11, VII. The Last Periods before the Division into Sexes, §§ 7–10](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c07.html)
- <a id='finding-ep07'></a> **EP07 · Fire mist, upright form and divided guidance.** Thinking organs are prepared in the fire mist. After lunar separation and cooling, Steiner links upstanding posture and hand rudiments with altered functions of the upper body. He assigns lower-body guidance to Moon beings and renewed influence over developed personality to Luciferic beings. [GA 11, X. Extrusion of the Moon, §§ 3–6](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c10.html)
- <a id='finding-ep08'></a> **EP08 · Moon separation, form and generation.** GA 13 describes the withdrawal of hardening forces as making individual embodiment possible. It relates emerging sexual differentiation to the distribution of seed and quickening capacities and describes lunar forces continuing from outside Earth. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 69–71, 78](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- <a id='finding-ep09'></a> **EP09 · Breath, warmth and early bodily systems.** GA 13 attributes guidance of breath into the body to Sun beings. It distinguishes warmth currents as beginnings of blood circulation from an internal differentiation of air as a basis of the later nervous system. These are claimed antecedents, with no implication of modern anatomical completion. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 71–73](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- <a id='finding-ep10'></a> **EP10 · Interrupted embodiment, I and return.** Some souls can no longer inhabit hardening forms and continue elsewhere. Spirits of Form kindle the I, whose experience carries into body-free existence. As suitable forms become available after Moon separation, formerly displaced souls return over a long interval. The account distinguishes these first returns from repeat Earth incarnations. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 74, 77–78, 87](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- <a id='finding-ep11'></a> **EP11 · Lunar organization and two adverse influences.** The Moon beings shape the relation of I and bodies. Luciferic intervention grants greater control of consciousness images alongside error and dependence on astral impulses. Steiner separately introduces Ahrimanic influence through earthly forces and fear before its stronger mid-Atlantean manifestation. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 80–82, 88](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- <a id='finding-ep12'></a> **EP12 · Different nighttime agencies act concurrently.** With alternating day and night, Steiner differentiates the nighttime sphere of activity around I and astral body from the simultaneous activity around physical and life bodies. Spirits of Form are common to both lists. This describes an early Earth rhythm, with a specific allocation of agencies rather than exclusive builders. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 85–87](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- <a id='finding-ep13'></a> **EP13 · Will, memory, speech and differentiated guidance.** GA 11 describes will-directed schooling and temple practice, and connects late Lemurian memory and elementary moral concepts to an account of women's role and of rhythmic utterance. The source uses gender essentialism and evolutionary ranking. Its local continuity claim reaches into early Atlantis while the fourth subgroup is in preparation. [GA 11, V. The Lemurian Race, §§ 2, 6–13, 18](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c05.html)
- <a id='finding-ep14'></a> **EP14 · Volcanic destruction and a quieter precursor settlement.** GA 11 depicts volcanism destroying Lemurian territory while a relatively quiet area supports settlement and formation of an Atlantean precursor group. The account distinguishes the destructive environment from development in the refuge. [GA 11, V. The Lemurian Race, §§ 14–16](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c05.html)
- <a id='finding-cp33'></a> **CP33 · Early soul-member differentiation and the I .** GA 13 distinguishes an early Earth differentiation of sentient, intellectual and consciousness soul in the astral constitution from the culmination that allows the Spirits of Form to kindle the I. Its German §77 names Bewusstseinsseele where the Adams translation uses spiritual soul. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 71, 77](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- <a id='finding-cp40'></a> **CP40 · I-work on the astral body begins in dim consciousness.** GA 104 begins partially unconscious activity of the I on the astral body in Lemuria and early Atlantis, yielding what this translation calls the sensory soul. Later application of the I to other members is treated in the same lecture as a sequence. [GA 104, Ninth Lecture, §§ 6–7](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080626p01.html)
- <a id='finding-cp44'></a> **CP44 · Early Earth bodily currents and preparatory organ forms.** Steiner describes etheric humans condensing into mineral-bearing bodies and claims Earth's prior currents continue inward as blood vessels and nerves. He treats the modern umbilical cord as an analogue of a former bond with Earth and names preparatory forms of heart and brain. These are esoteric analogies and developmental claims. [GA 104, Ninth Lecture, §§ 10–15](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080626p01.html)

### Memory, organization, independent thinking and transition (`earth-atlant`)

Read the seven overlapping phases in GA 11 III alongside the broad account of altered life-body, oracle and sensory experience in GA 13 IV. The sources provide no exact alignment for every process.

- <a id='finding-ep15'></a> **EP15 · Overlapping phases and memory-led naming.** Subgroups in Steiner's early taxonomy persist alongside successors. The first named subgroup emphasizes retained sense impressions, attachment to past experience and the development of naming already incipient in late Lemuria. [GA 11, III. Our Atlantean Ancestors, §§ 7–11](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c03.html)
- <a id='finding-ep16'></a> **EP16 · Second phase and shared memory of deeds.** Steiner describes remembered personal achievement as a new basis of leadership and ancestor veneration. Recalled joint undertakings could bind groups in addition to ties of descent. [GA 11, III. Our Atlantean Ancestors, §§ 12–13](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c03.html)
- <a id='finding-ep17'></a> **EP17 · Third phase, inherited authority and experimental settlement.** The third phase consolidates communal institutions, transmits leadership across generations, and uses remembered experience and experiments when establishing communities. Steiner sees an opening for personal appropriation of nature powers in the same process. [GA 11, III. Our Atlantean Ancestors, §§ 14–16](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c03.html)
- <a id='finding-ep18'></a> **EP18 · Fourth and fifth phases change the role of power.** Steiner places intensified egoistic use of life forces in the fourth phase and the use of comparison and judgment to regulate desire in the fifth. He describes a tradeoff between inward combinatory thought and immediate command of living forces. [GA 11, III. Our Atlantean Ancestors, §§ 17–19](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c03.html)
- <a id='finding-ep19'></a> **EP19 · Sixth and seventh phases differ in uses of thinking.** The sixth phase applies thinking more broadly to social order and law. The seventh is depicted as retaining greater attachment to inherited memory and belief in life powers while also developing thought. The source's extension of this typology to living populations is unsupported racial stereotyping. [GA 11, III. Our Atlantean Ancestors, §§ 20–22](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c03.html)
- <a id='finding-ep20'></a> **EP20 · Pictorial memory, education and seed-force technology.** GA 11 presents imitation and remembered cases as a basis of judgment and education, with initiates treated separately. It claims growth forces in seeds could be redirected to devices under different environmental conditions. These are esoteric technological claims without archaeological or physical verification. [GA 11, III. Our Atlantean Ancestors, §§ 1–5](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c03.html)
- <a id='finding-ep21'></a> **EP21 · Planetary affinities, sleep and distinct oracle practices.** GA 13 places the fuller differentiation of planetary affiliations in Atlantis. It distinguishes what sleepers and different kinds of initiates could experience through portions of the life-body, and calls the Christ oracle a prophetic stream within this wider field. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 91–94](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- <a id='finding-ep22'></a> **EP22 · Bodily stabilization and the mid-Atlantean crisis.** GA 13 connects bodily stabilization with variable plasticity, while naming misuse of growth and reproductive powers, including by some initiates, as a cause of destructive air and water changes. It separately emphasizes Ahrimanic distortion of sensory and postmortem experience from mid-Atlantis. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 95–97](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- <a id='finding-ep23'></a> **EP23 · Oracle preservation and the brain as instrument of thought.** The Sun oracle is credited with keeping and passing on etheric patterns of initiates. Among a successor group Steiner describes closer physical–etheric union, diminished older memory and the transformation of the brain into a needed instrument of thought and bodily self-awareness. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 98–99](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- <a id='finding-ep24'></a> **EP24 · Manu, independent understanding and a new type of initiate.** GA 11 describes selection of an eastward successor group, spiritual ideas addressed to its own thinking, and training of earthly thinkers as initiates. Later communities take on different forms as that teaching blends with older local inheritances. [GA 11, IV. Transition of the Fourth into the Fifth Root Race, §§ 4–8, 10–12](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c04.html)
- <a id='finding-ep25'></a> **EP25 · Two catastrophe narratives have distinct claimed mechanisms.** The Lemurian transition is connected to I-related use of fire under Luciferic influence. The Atlantean transition is linked to misuse of growth and generation under different conditions, with air and water effects and further migration. Both accounts are Steiner's esoteric prehistory. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 90, 96–99](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- <a id='finding-cp39'></a> **CP39 · The fifth-phase migration and bodily self-awareness.** This lecture begins an eastward migration in the fifth Atlantean phase, calls the formation of a successor group a last-third process, and connects physical–etheric change with capacity for first-person self-awareness. - <a id='finding-cp41'></a> **CP41 · Etheric and physical work extend over later Atlantean phases.** The lecture places dim I-work on the etheric body during Atlantis, giving rise to the intellectual soul, and work on the physical body from the last third, giving rise to the consciousness soul. [GA 104, Ninth Lecture, §§ 7](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080626p01.html)
- <a id='finding-cp45'></a> **CP45 · Atlantean heart rudiment in the lion group-soul account.** GA 104 connects a heart rudiment that later becomes the physical heart with the lion group-soul image. [GA 104, Ninth Lecture, §§ 20–22](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080626p01.html)

It later reuses their animal-head imagery in its apocalyptic treatment, with an altered meaning. [GA 104, Ninth Lecture, §§ 19–21](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080626p01.html)

### Post-Atlantean Earth epoch (`earth-post`)

The seven cultural periods are nested within this Earth epoch. Dates describe Steiner’s typology and some are inferred using his period arithmetic. GA 13 allows cultural continuities across the boundaries.

- <a id='finding-cp24'></a> **CP24 · Earth epochs nested inside the physical form condition.** GA 104 places the seven great Earth epochs inside the fourth form stage of Earth's fourth condition of life, numbered 172 in this lecture. The seven post-Atlantean cultures belong to the fifth of those Earth epochs. The local 3–4–4 tally refers to completed subdivisions at three depths. [GA 104, Eleventh Lecture, §§ 6–8, 14](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080629p01.html)
- <a id='finding-cp31'></a> **CP31 · Period arithmetic and the precision of calendar labels.** GA 184 calculates 2,160 years from its two stated epoch boundaries and obtains a midpoint by halving the interval. This supports the period length as Steiner's arithmetic convention. Dates obtained by projecting the interval onto other cultures are editorial derivatives, with a calendar-year-zero ambiguity around the BCE/CE crossover. [GA 184, Lecture Four, §§ 747 BCE / 1413 and 2,160-year calculation](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA184/English/RSP1985/19181011p02.html)
- <a id='finding-cp43'></a> **CP43 · Angels mediate individual and collective work.** GA 121 distinguishes individual work across incarnations from collective impulses. It describes Angels mediating between individual people and Archangels, with Spirits of Personality introducing a further influence at the scale of an age. The lecture's national character and ancestry claims are unsupported. [GA 121, Third Lecture, §§ 17–20, 22–23](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA121/English/SOL/19100609p01.html)

### Sixth great Earth epoch (`earth-sixth`)

GA 104 places the seven seals here after the War of All Against All. GA 104a treats early seals as astral images of transformed cultural fruits and sometimes moves back to the prewar cultures while discussing them.

- <a id='finding-cp25'></a> **CP25 · Seals follow a transition beyond the seventh culture.** The lecture locates Philadelphia within the present set of cultural periods, the War of All Against All after that set, and the seven seals after the war. The seals and trumpets each have their own further division in this particular account. [GA 104, Eleventh Lecture, §§ 7–8, 17](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080629p01.html)
- <a id='finding-cp26'></a> **CP26 · The first five seals and transformed cultural fruits.** GA 104a uses horse-and-rider pictures for the first four postwar periods, relating them to the first four cultures. The fifth period is pictured through the white robe and an astral body illuminated by the I. The source marks a gap at the end of the discussion of the fifth seal. [GA 104a, Ninth Lecture (Oslo), §§ 1–6](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104a/English/SOL/19090518p01.html)
- <a id='finding-cp27'></a> **CP27 · Philadelphia and the sixth seal occupy different scales.** The lecture moves between a prewar cultural period associated with Philadelphia, its projected interventions in air and light, and an image of their fruits at the sixth seal. The claim that electrical waves and moral actions will cause earthquakes is unsupported. [GA 104a, Ninth Lecture (Oslo), §§ 7–13](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104a/English/SOL/19090518p01.html)
- <a id='finding-cp30'></a> **CP30 · Post-catastrophe choice, altered conditions and distant lunar work.** GA 13 projects seven periods after the convulsion and treats prior choices as preparation for altered spiritual conditions. It then describes lunar return and transformative assistance farther ahead, without supplying the seal-by-seal concordance or dating lunar return to a specific seal. [GA 13, VI. Present and Future Evolution of the World and of Mankind, §§ 7–8](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c06.html)
- <a id='finding-cp37'></a> **CP37 · War of All Against All as a moral transition.** The 1924 lecture places the war between great Earth epochs five and six, after cultures six and seven within the fifth. Steiner gives the moral catastrophe primacy and describes accompanying natural events as secondary. The comparison with ice-age events belongs to his teaching. [GA 346, Sixth Lecture, §§ 18–19](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240910.html)
- <a id='finding-cp51'></a> **CP51 · From the white robes to the seventh seal.** Steiner recaps the fifth and sixth postwar periods, then describes the seventh seal through divergent conditions: one group can act from an invisible sphere while another remains bound to matter. He introduces the Angels' earlier developmental path as an analogy for the first group's future capacities. [GA 104a, Tenth Lecture (Oslo), §§ 1–2](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104a/English/SOL/19090519p01.html)
- <a id='finding-cp52'></a> **CP52 · Atlantis recapitulated as consciously regained clairvoyance.** In this lecture the seals era as a whole recapitulates Atlantis through consciously acquired clairvoyance. GA 104a II-9 additionally links individual seals with fruits of earlier post-Atlantean cultures. These correspondences have different chronological scales. [GA 104a, Tenth Lecture (Oslo), §§ 5](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104a/English/SOL/19090519p01.html)

[GA 104, Eleventh Lecture, §§ 2–5](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080629p01.html)
- <a id='finding-cp42'></a> **CP42 · Completion of conscious astral transformation and the last trumpet.** Steiner distinguishes present beginnings of Manas from advanced spiritual self-development at the end of physical Earth. He connects survival into the astralized Earth with transformed etheric organization and interprets Pauline imagery in that context. [GA 104, Ninth Lecture, §§ 6, 8–9](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080626p01.html)
- <a id='finding-cp49'></a> **CP49 · Human spiritual fruits prepare Future Jupiter.** GA 104 compares Earth's previous preparation of bodily forms with the future formative influence of what people accomplish through soul and etheric transformation. - <a id='finding-cp53'></a> **CP53 · Trumpet images and continuing physical conditions.** Steiner places the trumpets at a Devachanic level of hearing while describing a remaining Earth below. He explicitly numbers the first three images and the sixth. The darkening and first woe material sits between them in the prose and requires careful comparison before assigning every image a number. [GA 104a, Tenth Lecture (Oslo), §§ 3–4](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104a/English/SOL/19090519p01.html)
- <a id='finding-cp54'></a> **CP54 · Individuality, spiritual fire and recurrence of Lemuria.** Steiner links receiving the Christ impulse with accepting distinct individualities and emerging from conditions of group soul. He compares the trumpet era with a spiritually transformed Lemuria, connecting its fire imagery with a higher rather than material condition. [GA 104a, Tenth Lecture (Oslo), §§ 4–5](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104a/English/SOL/19090519p01.html)
- <a id='finding-cp55'></a> **CP55 · Two directions of reunion at the seventh trumpet.** The lecture projects a division in which the subtle portion of Earth reunites with the Sun while the dense portion combines with the Moon. Steiner calls it a transformed echo of the earlier solar separation. The resulting sequence requires comparison with GA 13's differently phrased reunion account. [GA 104a, Tenth Lecture (Oslo), §§ 5](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104a/English/SOL/19090519p01.html)
- <a id='finding-cp56'></a> **CP56 · Christ as planetary spirit and Sorat as a named adversary.** Steiner links Zarathustra's earlier teaching, Golgotha and Christ's projected role in reunion with the Sun. He calls Sorat a solar adversary affecting the astral body and presents 666 as a name-number correspondence in his reading of Revelation. [GA 104a, Tenth Lecture (Oslo), §§ 6–7](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104a/English/SOL/19090519p01.html)

### Ancient Indian cultural period (`period-10`)

Preserved Atlantean perception and its teaching provide a baseline for GA 13’s cultural account. The account explicitly distinguishes the first period from later India.

- <a id='finding-cp01'></a> **CP01 · Atlantean inheritance, teachers and cultural overlap.** GA 13 describes the seven disciples of the Christ-initiate teaching people receptive to residual Atlantean perception. It explicitly distinguishes those people from later historical India and lets Indian cultural development continue concurrently with the next two dominant periods. Its claims about caste and spiritual descent belong to a historical esoteric account. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 100–102](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- <a id='finding-cp02'></a> **CP02 · Etheric emphasis in the individual-development stream.** GA 185 assigns etheric development to the earliest post-Atlantean individual in its second current, alongside an independent account of the age at which natural maturing ceases. This is a typological emphasis and has no bearing on established human biology. [GA 185, Lecture Eight, §§ 3–5, 7](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA185/English/RSP1976/19181102p01.html)

### Ancient Persian cultural period (`period-11`)

Earthly work and the visibility of spiritual activity in the sensory world replace earlier emphases in Steiner’s typology. Ancient Persian spiritual content is projected to recur in transformed form in the sixth period.

- <a id='finding-cp03'></a> **CP03 · Persian orientation to work on Earth and the spirits of light.** Steiner portrays the sensory world as a field of action charged with spiritual significance. He distinguishes his primordial Zarathustra from later bearers of the name and associates the teaching about light with resisting Ahrimanic influence after death. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 103–104, 107](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- <a id='finding-cp04'></a> **CP04 · The sentient body in the individual-development stream.** GA 185 assigns the sentient body to the second cultural period at the scale of typical individual development. The passage separately assigns the sentient soul to the following period at that same scale. [GA 185, Lecture Eight, §§ 5, 7](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA185/English/RSP1976/19181102p01.html)

### Egypto-Chaldean cultural period (`period-12`)

Steiner groups several cultural settings in one typology while describing different initiation streams. The fifth culture is said to revisit earlier spiritual content with awakened individual faculties.

- <a id='finding-cp05'></a> **CP05 · Different initiatory responses to sensory immersion.** GA 13 describes related but distinct Egyptian and Chaldean-Babylonian cultural settings and ties increasing sensory interests to changing experience after death. Hermes is credited with preparing people during earthly life for the spiritual world, within Steiner's account of declining postmortem vision. [GA 13, IV. Man and the Evolution of the World, §§ 105, 107–109](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c04.html)
- <a id='finding-cp06'></a> **CP06 · Celestial observation and the Pergamum image.** GA 104 describes Chaldean observation of star motions as recognition of an intelligible pattern in visible nature. Pergamum represents this cultural period in the lecture's interpretive scheme. [GA 104, Third Lecture, §§ 10, 20](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080620p01.html)
- <a id='finding-cp07'></a> **CP07 · Sentient soul in one stream and a later analogy in another.** GA 185 assigns the sentient soul to the Egyptian-Chaldean period in its individual stream. In the separate whole-humanity stream, Steiner also calls fifth-age humanity analogous to the individual age band he associates with that same soul member. [GA 185, Lecture Eight, §§ 4–5, 7](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA185/English/RSP1976/19181102p01.html)

### Greco-Roman cultural period (`period-13`)

The mind or intellectual soul is the development foregrounded at the individual scale. GA 181 subdivides the period into three approximate intervals. GA 201 traces the cultural and Christological currents through the same time.

- **Timing and mechanism of reproductive differentiation** (open). GA 11 VI §§2–5 foregrounds densification and redirected soul force. He gives Michael and freely directed human spiritual activity a role in the outcome. The lecture presents an alternative adverse future contingent on human action without assigning a calendar date. [GA 346, Eighteenth Lecture, §§ 13–16](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240922.html)
- **NY18 · Sacramental reading of the three hierarchies.** The closing lecture interprets transubstantiation as an encounter with the hidden work of the first hierarchy in physical substance, with Christ leading toward a second-hierarchy disclosure and a region associated with the third hierarchy and the Holy Spirit. This is a sacramental account of spiritual perception. [GA 346, Eighteenth Lecture, §§ 20–24](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240922.html)

### GA 104 X–XI and GA 11 XI–XII · Consciousness, transitions and ordinal chronology

- **NY19 · Old Saturn and variable trance-like awareness.** Steiner assigns the rudimentary human organization a predominant awareness duller than dreamless sleep. He explicitly allows variation within Saturn and says other beings then possessed what he calls human consciousness. [GA 104, Tenth Lecture, §§ 4–8](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080627p01.html)
- **NY20 · Old Sun and the dreamless-sleep analogue.** Steiner compares the predominant Old Sun awareness to present dreamless sleep and to the condition he attributes to plants. These are analogies within his account of consciousness. [GA 104, Tenth Lecture, §§ 6](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080627p01.html)
- **NY21 · Old Moon image awareness and its Atlantean reprise.** Steiner describes vivid images corresponding to the spiritual environment, distinguishes them from fragmented present dreams, and says this condition was repeated during Atlantean evolution as Earth developed waking object consciousness. [GA 104, Tenth Lecture, §§ 7–8](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080627p01.html)
- **NY22 · Jupiter preserves waking awareness in conscious imaging.** In Steiner’s projection, Jupiter consciousness combines image perception with the clear awareness proper to Earth. He describes initiation as anticipating this combination. [GA 104, Tenth Lecture, §§ 9–10](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080627p01.html)
- **NY23 · Venus and inspired tonal consciousness.** Steiner projects an inspired awareness in which an inner character appears as a unified tone and beings formerly experienced as images are perceived as tonal structures. [GA 104, Tenth Lecture, §§ 11](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080627p01.html)
- **NY24 · Vulcan and intuitive consciousness with individuality.** Steiner calls the projected Vulcan state intuition in a technical sense. The soul participates inwardly in beings within its field of perception and retains its individuality. [GA 104, Tenth Lecture, §§ 11](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080627p01.html)
- **NY25 · Plant forces, laboratory creation and future ethics.** Steiner projects that understanding plant growth forces will allow the creation of plant forms without seeds. He frames legitimate use as requiring a transformed moral life and links the projected kingdom to shared well-being. These are esoteric forecasts. [GA 104, Tenth Lecture, §§ 15–21](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080627p01.html)
- **NY26 · The three completed counts in Steiner’s 344.** Steiner counts three completed forms of the fourth life condition, four completed great epochs of the physical form and four completed post-Atlantean cultural periods. He reads the three digits individually. [GA 104, Eleventh Lecture, §§ 14](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080629p01.html)
- **NY27 · 66, 466 and 666 in the longer trumpet account.** Steiner puts 66 after six trumpets and treats the seventh-trumpet disposition, associated with 466, as still reversible for a time. He reserves final separation for a later 666. The lecture’s adjacent descriptions use different scale terminology for that last formula. [GA 104, Eleventh Lecture, §§ 15–18](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080629p01.html)
- **NY28 · Conditional prophecy and free choice.** In the authored essay, Steiner distinguishes the purported lawfulness of a result from the free choices that may establish its conditions. He presents spiritual foresight as compatible with freely brought-about events and claims it can guide ideals. [GA 11, XI. Some Necessary Points of View, §§ 4–7](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c11.html)
- **NY29 · Seven embodiments and living intervals of rest.** Steiner presents humanity and its sphere as co-developing through three past, Earth and three future embodiments. In the intervening pralayas life persists, though conditions elude the senses developed in the open cycles. [GA 11, XII. On the Origin of the Earth, §§ 1–5](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c12.html)
- **NY30 · Saturn awareness is dim in clarity and broad in reach.** Steiner distinguishes the breadth of awareness from its clarity. He describes Saturn as broader and dimmer than Earth, and distinguishes a trance comparison from a clairvoyant’s claimed retention of waking clarity. [GA 11, XII. On the Origin of the Earth, §§ 6–9](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c12.html)
- **NY31 · Recapitulated awareness changes form on Sun and Moon.** Steiner has Sun develop the Saturn predisposition again in changed form before its own awareness. Moon subsequently repeats the Saturn and Sun stages in smaller cycles before attaining an image awareness keyed to its surroundings. [GA 11, XII. On the Origin of the Earth, §§ 9–10](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA011/English/RSPI1959/GA011_c12.html)

### GA 346 I–V · Human constitution, mystery chronology and cultural consciousness

- **NY32 · Older mystery rites and withdrawal of direct divine presence.** Steiner describes a claimed ancient mode of direct contact between gods and officiants, tied to celestial timing. In his semi-ancient mode the gods send powers and the officiants use preserved substances as a means of ritual knowledge. The passage gives a relative order. [GA 346, First Lecture, §§ 15–17, 23–25](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240905.html)
- **NY33 · Four levels of ritual reception and the conscious I.** Steiner connects the first three mystery modes to physical, etheric and astral experience. He situates the first Communion within the third mode and describes the modern task as consciously uniting the I with Christ and grasping the rite and revelation. [GA 346, Second Lecture, §§ 30–32](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240906.html)
- **NY34 · Saturn warmth boundary and the experience of wonder.** Steiner depicts early warmth differentiation as a warmer interior and a cooler warmth envelope. He calls its inward experience wonder and uses Alpha and Beta to represent warmth-human and first enclosure. [GA 346, Third Lecture, §§ 18–22](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240907.html)
- **NY35 · Vulcan completion and Christ at Earth’s midpoint.** Steiner interprets Alpha and Omega as the initial human being united with its whole world at Vulcan. He describes Christ at Golgotha as already at the stage humanity would reach at that endpoint. [GA 346, Third Lecture, §§ 23–24](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240907.html)
- **NY36 · Twenty-four Elders and the four living figures.** Steiner interprets the Elders as enduring revelations of successive representatives, including some still to come, assembled around Christ. The four living figures signify humanity as a whole in this scene. The passage supplies no dated sequence of twenty-four eras. [GA 346, Fourth Lecture, §§ 32–37](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240908.html)
- **NY37 · Loss of remembered pre-existence and persistence of fourth-period habits.** Steiner attributes awareness of pre-earthly life to the first three cultural periods and a growing sense of earthly boundedness to the fourth. He says fourth-period habits persist into the fifth through education and public life. [GA 346, Fifth Lecture, §§ 8–10](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240909.html)
- **NY38 · Consciousness soul and an awareness of continuous dying.** Steiner predicts that people will increasingly experience dying as active throughout life. His fire image concerns the felt transition into wakefulness and the inward cost of awareness. The reference belongs to his phenomenology of consciousness. [GA 346, Fifth Lecture, §§ 11–12, 22–23](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240909.html)
- **NY39 · Sixth cultural period and changed perception of nature.** Steiner distinguishes the fifth period’s change in self-perception from a sixth-period change in how the surroundings are experienced. He interprets falling stars in terms of awareness of spiritual activity behind the visible heavens. [GA 346, Fifth Lecture, §§ 24–27](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240909.html)
- **NY40 · Sardis as the fifth church and fifth seal in the smaller reading.** Steiner applies the Sardis image and the fifth seal to the modern fifth cultural period and urges his priestly audience to realize the task in daily life. GA 346 XIII separately explains the smaller scale used in this course. [GA 346, Fifth Lecture, §§ 41](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240909.html)

### GA 104 VIII–IX and GA 121 IV · Earth transitions, the I and hierarchy activity

- **NY41 · The free I as a capacity for love and hardening.** The lecture traces the I to differentiation from group-soul consciousness. Steiner interprets the two-edged sword as the capacity to deepen free individuality or harden it into selfish conflict. He assigns the War of All Against All to the second trajectory. [GA 104, Eighth Lecture, §§ 1–4](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080625p01.html)
- **NY42 · The sixth cultural period prepares a later community.** Steiner associates Philadelphia with spiritual brotherhood and treats souls formed in the sixth cultural period as a foundation of post-war cultures. He describes the transition through an analogy with his account of the end of Atlantis. [GA 104, Eighth Lecture, §§ 5, 9–13](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080625p01.html)
- **NY43 · A reversible separation and the future educational task.** Steiner assigns a future effort to draw back separated people to those he calls disciples of Manichaeism. He still allows recovery at the astral transition. The passage records his reconstruction of Manichaeism and its projected role. [GA 104, Eighth Lecture, §§ 10–12, 25](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080625p01.html)
- **NY44 · Late Lemurian to Atlantean migration in the 1908 account.** Steiner distinguishes early ethereal Earth conditions from the later epochs and attributes the founding of Atlantean populations to an emigrating minority of the last Lemurian group. This is an esoteric prehistory with racialized terminology. [GA 104, Eighth Lecture, §§ 14–15](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080625p01.html)
- **NY45 · Christ-derived love and the astralization of Earth.** Steiner associates the physical-to-astral transition with the capacity to dissolve material form through love. He interprets seven bowls of wrath through the expulsion of hardened matter. The account places this transition before its final irreversible outcome. [GA 104, Eighth Lecture, §§ 16–20, 23–25](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080625p01.html)
- **NY46 · Transformation of the three bodies through the I.** Steiner calls the I’s transformation of the astral body Spirit Self, of the etheric body Life Spirit and of the physical body Spirit Man. Earth permits development of these members, while their full development belongs to a more distant future. [GA 104, Ninth Lecture, §§ 2–5](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080626p01.html)
- **NY47 · The separated Earth and the horns as hardened limbs.** After the trumpets Steiner describes an astral Earth and a separate portion unable to participate in its transformation. His reading of the beast’s horns treats them as hardened physical limbs of that portion’s inhabitants. [GA 104, Ninth Lecture, §§ 1, 23](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080626p01.html)
- **NY48 · Spirits of Form, retarded Spirits of Movement and early awareness.** Steiner credits the Spirits of Form with bestowing the capacity for I-consciousness. He assigns earlier perception of the physical environment in individual development to Spirits of Movement that act at the rank of Form. Ages and stages belong to the lecture’s esoteric account. [GA 121, Fourth Lecture, §§ 2–6](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA121/English/SOL/19100610p01.html)
- **NY49 · Lemurian bodily differentiation and a cultural-repetition thesis.** Steiner associates Lemurian bodily differentiation with locality and reproduction under beings he calls abnormal Spirits of Form. He then describes post-Atlantean cultural stages as changed repetitions of previous bodily conditions. The racial and geographic claims are historically situated and unsupported. [GA 121, Fourth Lecture, §§ 7–8, 14–15](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA121/English/SOL/19100610p01.html)
- **NY50 · Early physical growth as a recurrence of Saturn.** Steiner connects the physical organization from birth to the change of teeth with an individual reprise of Old Saturn. He distinguishes that model of repeated physical development from the distinctive Earth work of the Spirits of Form on the I. [GA 121, Fourth Lecture, §§ 1–3](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA121/English/SOL/19100610p01.html)

### Source comparisons and edition checks

- **The historical trumpets and the later Earth trumpets belong to different scales** (qualified distinction). GA 346 XIII §3 explicitly distinguishes a consciousness-soul strand from the general human development addressed in 1908. Its smaller historical sequence treats the Crusades as the shift to trumpets. GA 104 XI places the great trumpets epoch after the seals and after the War of All Against All. Keep both readings and retain the explicit distinction of scope. Evidence NY01, NY03, NY04, CP28.
- **The 1940s wording is specific to the checked SOL English rendering** (qualified distinction). GA 346 XIII German §§4–6 gives the early forties and 1843–44 before continuing with the same forties. German §23 says the sixth trumpet is being experienced at the time of the 1924 talk. Reading §6 as 1840s follows the nearest antecedent and fits §23. The alternate English view also continues with the forties after explicitly naming the 1840s. Record the SOL English wording and edition provenance. Evidence NY02, NY04.
- **1933, 1998 and multiple modern Apocalypse datings** (open). GA 346 XVI §§21–24 names 1933 in a comet and beast discussion. VIII §§20–24 puts a Sorat marker at 1998 and XIII relates a seventh trumpet to the late twentieth century on its narrower scale. The images and assigned predicates differ. The lectures do not specify a precise relation between the 1933 and 1998 markers. Evidence NY11, NX06, NY04.
- **The internal scale of the ordinal 666 in GA 104 XI** (open). In §15 the sequence is described using life cycles and two subdivisions called great and small epochs. In §18 the last long phase is described by six completed life conditions and six forms within the seventh. The German confirms the change in terminology. The lecture clearly places 666 later than the trumpet transition, but the mapping of all three digits across these descriptions and to the date-bearing GA 346 sequence remains open. Evidence NY26, NY27, NX06.
- **The fifth seal and Sardis on two time scales** (qualified distinction). GA 346 V applies the fifth seal to the fifth cultural period alongside Sardis. GA 104 assigns the larger seven-seal sequence to the next great Earth epoch. GA 346 XIII expressly distinguishes its fourth- and fifth-period framework from the larger earlier reading. Evidence NY40, NY01, CP28.
- **Saturn as deep trance and as inward wonder** (open). GA 104 X qualifies its deep-trance comparison as predominant and GA 11 XII distinguishes reach from clarity. GA 346 III describes warmth and inward wonder within early Saturn. The sources do not explicitly identify the subject or subdivision that would make wonder and trance descriptions of one phenomenological state. Evidence NY19, NY30, NY34.

### GA 346 I · Mystery stages and ritual time

- **NZ01 · Interval of independent development and four mystery stages.** Steiner says direct mystery work once withdrew to leave humanity largely to its own resources. He claims that interval’s forces have become exhausted and presents modern ritual as a new stage. He deliberately introduces his four successive mystery modes without calendar dates. [GA 346, First Lecture, §§ 1–3, 8–14](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240905.html)
- **NZ02 · Recurring ritual times at calendar adjustments.** In the first mystery mode, Steiner places ritual access to deities at selected calendar discrepancies, including end-of-year intervals and an eighteen-year lunar cycle. He ascribes to priests the preservation of powers received at these times for use during the rest of the year. [GA 346, First Lecture, §§ 17–22](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240905.html)
- **NZ03 · From preserved ferments to the ritual Word.** Steiner distinguishes the solar radiance he attributes to the earlier vessels from the third mystery mode’s rhythmic ritual language and images in sacrificial smoke. He connects this third mode to Samothrace and describes later Masses as inheriting aspects of it. [GA 346, First Lecture, §§ 26–30](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240905.html)
- **NZ04 · Christian Renewal and the fourth ritual mode.** Addressing the new priesthood, Steiner ties the fourth mystery mode to its inauguration at the first Goetheanum roughly two years before the lecture. He presents the rite and Apocalypse as linked subjects in the proposed religious renewal. [GA 346, First Lecture, §§ 5–8, 31](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240905.html)

### GA 346 V · Cultural analogies, moral experience and the senses

- **NZ05 · Four churches as cultural continuations.** Steiner sees Ephesus as carrying elements of the first post-Atlantean period into Christianity, Smyrna as carrying the Persian inheritance, Pergamon as recalling the Hermetic third period and Thyatira as recalling the fourth period and Golgotha. These are typological associations. [GA 346, Fifth Lecture, §§ 3–5](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240909.html)
- **NZ06 · Sardis as a carried-forward seed and the pale horse of the fourth seal.** Steiner has the letter to Sardis carry an embryonic element for the fifth cultural period. He describes death entering human awareness under the fourth seal’s pale horse and reads the break in horse imagery as a sign of change in the fifth period. [GA 346, Fifth Lecture, §§ 6–7, 27–29](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240909.html)
- **NZ07 · Consciousness soul and moral weight.** Steiner predicts that unrealized intentions and some forms of intellectual activity will increasingly be felt as draining. His account treats moral weight as inward experience of fate and suggests why clergy might need to attend to individual experience. [GA 346, Fifth Lecture, §§ 13–16](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240909.html)
- **NZ08 · Sardis, attention to individuality and cultivation of will.** Steiner uses Sardis’s white garments as an image for perceiving the individual behind outward social circumstances. He asks his priestly audience to cultivate will through the Apocalypse and contrasts that proposal with his own polemical account of Jesuit training. [GA 346, Fifth Lecture, §§ 17–21, 42–44](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240909.html)
- **NZ09 · Olfaction, apperception and the Sardis example.** Steiner calls the human intellectual apparatus a transformed olfactory organ, singling out apperception, and attributes some plant fragrances to perceived planetary influences. This is his speculative example of stars at work within nature. The passage gives no evolutionary stage for the alleged organ transformation. [GA 346, Fifth Lecture, §§ 31–35](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240909.html)
- **NZ10 · Solar cycles in the account of plant and mineral colors.** Steiner distinguishes a daily, yearly and roughly 25,920-year solar cycle. He ascribes plant colors to the annual course and stone colors to the longest cycle, then calls on the audience to bring a sense of stellar agency and the solar Christ into the present. The color claims are esoteric. [GA 346, Fifth Lecture, §§ 36–40](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240909.html)

### GA 346 XIII · Historic transitions and what its numbers describe

- **NZ11 · The locust and one-third imagery in Steiner’s fifth and sixth trumpets.** Steiner reads the locusts and the later killing in Revelation through a claim that some people lack a human I. He discusses several hypothetical origins in his esoteric scheme. This is dehumanizing historical material with no warrant for identifying, assessing or classifying anyone. [GA 346, Thirteenth Lecture, §§ 7–13, 23](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240917.html)
- **NZ12 · Crusades and the oral transmission of cosmic Christianity.** Steiner describes earlier Christianity as centered on Gospel and liturgy and attributes a transmission of cosmic Christianity from East to West around the Crusades, mainly through oral tradition in his account. He places a seventh seal at this juncture. [GA 346, Thirteenth Lecture, §§ 17–20](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240917.html)
- **NZ13 · Numerical patterns at the threshold and collective figures.** Steiner treats seven as a method of observation and number as relevant at the threshold in his initiation model. He treats the sealed and saved figures as pertaining to his scheme of collective development and distinguishes them from individual destiny. [GA 346, Thirteenth Lecture, §§ 25–29](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA346/English/SOL/19240917.html)

### GA 104 XI · The central form, the two beasts and preparation for Jupiter

- **NZ14 · The central form and loss of Atlantean clairvoyance.** Steiner makes the current physical form central in a 343-form series while putting the present period beyond that form’s internal midpoint. He connects the displacement to a loss of older clairvoyance around mid-Atlantis and places the Christ impulse within the resulting spiritually dark interval. [GA 104, Eleventh Lecture, §§ 9–12](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080629p01.html)
- **NZ15 · Sorat and the two future beasts.** Steiner distinguishes those characterized by the seven-headed beast from the two-horned being he calls their seducer. He relates that agency to resistance to union with the Sun and to abuse of spiritual powers after the astral transition. [GA 104, Eleventh Lecture, §§ 19–20, 26–29](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080629p01.html)
- **NZ16 · Sorat’s name-number and four human members.** Steiner supplies a letter-number reading of 666 and relates the four signs to the physical, etheric and astral bodies and the lower I. The explanation describes symbolic retention and hardening of those members. [GA 104, Eleventh Lecture, §§ 21–25](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080629p01.html)
- **NZ17 · Michael, the New Jerusalem and preparatory forms of Jupiter.** Steiner relates New Jerusalem to human activity that prepares future Jupiter forms. He assigns to Michael, as the Solar Genius’s representative, the overcoming and binding of the adversary. These are linked into a projected planetary transition through his Apocalypse reading. [GA 104, Eleventh Lecture, §§ 30–31](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA104/English/SOL/19080629p01.html)
