## Cross lecture results

The 8 October account traces thought and memory to allegedly transformed childhood formative forces. This extends the comparison between GA313 IX §6 (v14) and GA315 VI §5 (v18): childhood formation, later cognition and adult organ plasticity are related claims with different endpoints, not interchangeable descriptions. The same lecture links retained organizing forces to cancer and childhood illness. Those etiologies are unvalidated; its measles/scarlet-fever account is scientifically incorrect as an infectious-disease explanation.

The morning 9 October lecture §3 expressly rejects a spatial partition at the neck and trunk. Its threefoldness is functional: nervous-sensory, rhythmic and metabolic-limb activities extend through the organism. In §4, feeling and will are assigned different primary bodily bases, while their representations involve nervous-sensory mediation. A cerebrospinal-fluid mechanism is specifically asserted for represented feeling. This clarifies terminology within Steiner's account without establishing its empirical validity.

The evening lecture §§5–11 differentiates I activity from its alleged physical blood-based framework. Its brain account treats prevented ossification and balanced incipient rickets as conditions of healthy activity. Do not index this as actual brain rickets or as a cosmic organ-origin date. The accompanying phosphorus claims are historical medical assertions involving a potentially toxic substance.

Its diabetes passage contains a concrete translation correction: German §23 says *Zuckerharnruhr*, continuing §§20–22, whereas SOL English says dysentery. The concordance indexes diabetes and retains the printed-English discrepancy. This correction establishes the topic, not the truth of the excessive-I mechanism or essential-oil bath proposal.

The reproductive account in §§25–27 distinguishes purported male/earthly/blood-I and female/extraterrestrial contributions, then invokes hemophilia inheritance as an illustration. An observed inheritance pattern does not verify that cosmology. The simplified male/female account is not a complete genetic account, and the register does not infer any named spiritual organ builder.
