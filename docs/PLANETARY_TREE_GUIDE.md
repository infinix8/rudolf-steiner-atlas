# Planetary Tree of Life

The [Tree of Life](../atlas/planetary-tree.html) uses the planetary placement from the [BOTA Living Tree reference](https://bota-living-tree-atlas.pages.dev/index.html?atlas=native&mode=major) as an editorial entry point into Steiner’s work. From top to bottom the planetary positions are Saturn at Binah, Jupiter at Chesed, Mars at Geburah, Sun at Tiphareth, Venus at Netzach, Mercury at Hod, Moon at Yesod and Earth at Malkuth. The upper centre opens the cosmic evolution guide. The upper right opens the source-linked zodiac. These two gateways extend the use of the layout. The tree’s lines support orientation. Evidence for each Steiner correspondence appears in the guide that opens from the node.

Earth has its own guide to the fourth embodiment and to human development there. The seven principal sphere guides draw on GA 110. Hidden Vulcan opens separately and distinguishes the Atlantean Oracle, the beings described in GA 204 and Future Vulcan. Mercury and Venus keep their source-local names in the guides, including the terminology comparison from GA 110.

The planetary dossiers use the [Living Cosmos graph](research/unified-macrocosm-microcosm/KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH.json) for original citations and evidence classes. The [editorial crosswalk](../research/planetary-tree-v1.json) adds explicit reading paths for related passages and several source-backed notes. A related study is a route to a topic. Its relevance to the planet follows the cited chain of relations. Direct statements, syntheses, and questions use the research graph’s evidence labels.

Topics progress from spiritual beings and cosmic evolution through human history, the interval between lives, soul faculties, organs, blood, and substances. Sections appear where the evidence supports them. The metal notes record the historical correspondences from [GA 232 XI §13](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA232/English/SOL/19231215p01.html) and [GA 316 III §13](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA316/English/SOL/19240423.html). Both explicitly include lunar silver. The Mars blood-and-iron note comes from [GA 231 III §§26–29](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA231/English/APC1961/19231117a01.html). These materials document Steiner’s writing and lecture contexts.

The Tree of Life is a navigation view for the existing research corpus. For wider material and open questions see [The Macrocosm and Microcosm](research/unified-macrocosm-microcosm/MASTER_RESEARCH.md) and [its research checkpoint](research/unified-macrocosm-microcosm/RESEARCH_RESUME.md).
