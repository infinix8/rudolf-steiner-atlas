# Iteration record — 2026-11-01

## User's direction

Build **on top of** (i.e. as an intellectual navigation layer over) RS Archive and the official Rudolf Steiner Archiv bibliography. Never duplicate or rehost the source texts. Prioritize deep categorization, systematic finding and conceptual connections. Accomplish substantial, testable work per iteration.

## Delivered

- Reviewed source sites' topic, GA and bibliographic organization and their separate identities.
- Established explicit complementary architecture and limits on copying/scraping.
- Implemented static source-first concept browser for the 1,006-node taxonomy (all six regions, 26 domains).
- Implemented concept search with domain filtering, breadcrumbs, local deep links, a nested-topic explorer, cross-domain connections and eight original **editorial** study routes.
- Connected the site's 14 original topic pages by a concept crosswalk; it links outward rather than reproducing lists.
- Added 11 located-but-not-fully-audited links to book chapters / dated lectures, with explicit evidence status.
- Added consistent disclaimers for GA volume-only leads and provisional semantic relationships.
- Added validation and CI (see tools).

## What is *not* complete

- The original 1,006 concept entries are a first-pass taxonomy, not evidence-checked across the GA corpus.
- The eight guided routes are editorial reading suggestions, not source-certified connections.
- The first 11 precise source links are located, but full passage citation, context and translation comparison still need a topic-by-topic audit.
- No actual full-text content, authenticated archive API, harvesting or backend search of source archives is implemented.
- The prototype is committed but not hosted as a public site, nor claimed to be browser-tested across devices.

## Highest-value next substantial iteration

1. Build an **all-GA coverage register** from bibliographic metadata, manually or with authorized data, mapping what is covered / missing without copying texts.
2. Prioritize terminology and doctrinal differences: etheric/life, astral/soul body, spirit-self / Manas, worldview labels and distinct threefold schemas.
3. Upgrade 30 prototype crosslinks to evidence-backed typed relations with dated lecture references and caveats.
4. Expand primary-source gateways to every subdomain, using exact book chapters or dated lectures.
5. Add source-aware concept comparison ('same term, different contexts'), timelines, and an atlas connection graph.
6. Perform accessibility, navigation and responsive QA in a real browser before deployment.
