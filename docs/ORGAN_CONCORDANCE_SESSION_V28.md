# Organ concordance research update · 5 November 2026

The English first pass of **GA 110, Spiritual Hierarchies**, now covers all ten lectures and both question-and-answer sessions. The closing lectures answer two different questions. The first asks where bodily form comes from. The second asks what a human being can eventually do with a bodily organization received from others. Steiner gives a collective account of the first and uses the ability to give to another being as his test of the second. ### Which beings made which organs?

In GA 110 IX §8 Steiner describes a series of *Anlagen*, or first dispositions, in the warmth condition of Old Saturn. The heart under Leo and the protective chest under Cancer repeat lecture VIII. Gemini concerns bilateral form, Aries the upper head and Taurus the organ of speech. In §9 he changes the question. He describes what a figure drawn on a mystery-school ceiling would show, including the arms for Gemini, the lower legs for Aquarius and the feet for Pisces. Only then does he name the Thrones, Seraphim and Cherubim together as the ranks whose forces formed the primordial human organization. The passage supports that collective attribution. An assignment of each rank to one organ would require another text. His comparison with Kabbalah here is specifically to Adam Kadmon. He does not give a mapping of the ten sefirot. [GA 110 IX, §§8–9](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/English/SOL/19090418a01.html)

In a lecture thirteen years later, Steiner assigns a different role to the human being. Between death and a new birth, the individual is said to participate with higher beings in preparing the inner forms of the eye and ear. Here the subject is an individual’s forthcoming embodiment, and the higher beings remain unnamed. Later in the same lecture the physically formed eye sees visible light, while the spiritual aspect of light is said to have formed it. This qualifies the much shorter formulation that light creates the eye in the 1909 question-and-answer notes. [GA 218, 9 December 1922, §§2, 13–15](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA218/English/SOL/19221209p01.html) · [GA 110, Q&A II, §10](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/English/SOL/19090422a01.html)

### What the I contributes

Steiner separates imprinting, transforming and giving. In lecture IX, the transformed astral organization can leave an imprint on the etheric body before that etheric body has itself been fully transformed. His stronger claim follows in §16. An advanced individuality able to form new astral and etheric bodies can, in his account, give previously developed ones to another. He illustrates this with Zarathustra and Hermes. He compares the act with the Thrones giving their own substance, while expressly distinguishing the degree. The passage goes beyond perception to a proposed creative capacity. Its object here is a supersensible vehicle. It supplies no method for constructing another person's anatomical organ. [GA 110 IX, §§13–16](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/English/SOL/19090418a01.html)

GA 107 gives an example that can be understood before accepting any of Steiner's cosmology. Two people encounter a situation that creates an obligation. One recognizes another person's need and responds. Steiner treats the response as something the person brings to an inherited situation. Alongside moral judgment he names logical and aesthetic activity, and says what people create this way can serve the Spirits of Personality. The bodily organizations received from earlier beings provide the conditions for that activity. In GA 102 he says that earlier beings build the sheaths and the I eventually works on them from within. [GA 107 XIX, §§10–12 and 15–23](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA107/English/SOL/19090617p01.html) · [GA 102 II, §§20–23](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA102/English/SOL/19080127p01.html)

### Blood, Christ and freedom

GA 110 X §18 gives the unusually direct link. Steiner treats blood as the material expression of the I and blood warmth as its elemental expression, tracing the latter to Old Saturn. He then identifies the being revealed to Moses in fire with the solar being embodied in Jesus. The next two sections interpret Christ's death as the decisive deed and its reception as a free act of the individual I. In §22 the direction of action matters: what the I freely receives is said to work outward into astral organization. In §§25–26 the ability to act otherwise makes love possible and gives the still-developing human hierarchy its distinctive task. These are successive claims about embodiment, a religious event and human agency. Blood physiology alone supplies none of the later steps. [GA 110 X, §§17–26](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/English/SOL/19090418p01.html)

### Four organs across two lives in GA 205

| Organ | Present-life relation in Steiner's account | What the interior is said to prepare | Element assigned in §21 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lungs | Surface reflects especially abstract thoughts into memory | External form of the subsequent head | Earth, with an express qualification about exclusivity |
| Liver | Surface reflects thoughts coloured by feeling | Inner dispositions of the subsequent brain | Water |
| Kidneys | Reflection has more to do with enduring emotional habits | Future temperament mediated by head organization | Air |
| Heart | Outer wall reflects experiences as pangs of conscience | Dispositions toward later karmic action | Warmth |

The four rows are linked to their earlier version 7 records. Steiner locates a mediating role in the metabolic-limb organization. He distinguishes surviving formative forces from physical tissues that decay. The table records a historical esoteric claim. In particular, his denial in §13 that the heart pumps blood is medically incorrect. [GA 205 VI, §§1, 5, 7, 10 and 12–16, 21](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA205/English/SOL/19210702p01.html) · [NHLBI on the circulation of blood](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart/blood-flow)

GA 102 I illustrates why the verb matters. There Steiner describes Saturn beings as finding expression in the **liver** and acting on it in the present. He connects the **earliest senses** with the Saturn stage in the very next paragraph. His account of the **lungs** emphasizes the exchange of air with the outside world. GA 205 later associates the lung *as an organ* especially with earth. The texts give different subjects and predicates: breathing exchanges air, an organ has an elemental affinity, beings exercise a present influence, and a disposition arises at a primordial stage. The older GA 128 liver–Jupiter correspondence remains a separate entry. [GA 102 I, §§24–30](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA102/English/SOL/19080106p01.html) · [GA 205 VI, §§20–22](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA205/English/SOL/19210702p01.html)

### Corrections that affect retrieval

- **GA 205 VI §18.** The German repeatedly uses *Tobsucht*, an older term for frenzy or raving. The SOL English shifts from rage to rabies and epilepsy. A search for either modern diagnosis would return a false attribution. The historical explanation through karmic forces also lacks clinical standing. [German §18](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA205/German/19210702.html) · [English §18](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA205/English/SOL/19210702p01.html)
- **GA 110 IX §§17–18.** Both online texts shift from the Moon to Mars when discussing where angels passed through a human stage. The inconsistency survives comparison with German. A printed-text check is still needed. [German §§17–18](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/German/19090418a.html)
- **GA 110 Q&A I §§29–35.** The German question concerns Vulcan as a future stage. The English heading suggests volcanic activity. In §§33–35, blood refers to kinship. The short numbered §35 finishes the preceding sentence. [German Q&A I](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA110/German/19090421p.html)
- **GA 102 II §22.** The German says that earlier beings prepared *Hüllen*, sheaths that would receive the I. The English wording “physical bodies” narrows the referent. [German §22](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA102/German/19080127.html)
- **GA 218 §24.** Steiner's spiritual eye after death belongs under comprehension and development. Here he says that a person can form it by thoughtfully understanding reported spiritual findings. In §§2 and 14 the subject is instead the formation of the physical eye before birth. [GA 218, §§2, 14 and 23–24](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA218/English/SOL/19221209p01.html)
