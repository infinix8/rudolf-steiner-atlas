How to interpret these relationships
  1. Cosmic stages such as Old Saturn and Old Sun differ from today's physical planets.
  2. A spirit's alleged sphere, earlier evolutionary phase, formative task, and zodiacal direction are different predicates.
  3. The words physical body and organ may concern a primordial rudiment or an operative spiritual agent rather than mature human anatomy.
  4. Different ranks can cooperate simultaneously: a plan, its realization, and the preservation of the result need not be assigned to one being.
  5. The archived English language may use overlapping names such as Powers; where relevant, retain German and Latin rank names.
