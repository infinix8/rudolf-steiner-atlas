Read the analogy correctly

A map of relationships, not a map of causes.

Steiner's names describe supposed etheric and occult relationships between organ processes. They are not modern physiology, planetary radiation measurements, or proof that one hierarchy alone builds one organ. The surrounding hierarchies and broader bodily systems must be studied separately.

The exception

And what of hidden Vulcan?

No corresponding organ is named for Vulcan in the reviewed GA 107 / GA 128 seven-organ teachings. Its secret Atlantean Oracle, later 'Vulcan beings', and future cosmic embodiment must remain separate studies.

Historical text ≠ medical guidance

GA 128 also invokes historical metal analogies (for example, gold, tin, lead, iron, copper and mercury). These have not been established as safe or effective treatments; lead and mercury can be toxic. Do not use planet–organ correspondences for diagnosis or treatment.
