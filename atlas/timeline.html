Chronology of linked original lectures

Follow the dated lectures that have contributed evidence to the Atlas. For authored books and essays, a lecture date is not automatically a publication date—those sources are shown separately without an invented date.

The chronological placement represents **the dates of the original talks**, not the date Steiner first developed an idea, its priority over other teachings, or its independent historical validity. The graph is a selective research sample and not the complete lecture corpus.
