The Atlantean Oracles

A cross-stage spiritual layer: seven initiation streams, not a one-to-one list of Atlantean peoples.

How to interpret the stages

  • Lemuria: seven thematic analytical milestones, some overlapping—not seven independently attested chronological subdivisions.
  • Atlantis: seven labels found in Steiner’s early GA 11 narrative, using historical esoteric terminology that must not be applied to modern ethnic groups.
  • Post-Atlantis: seven cultural periods; the last two are Steiner’s descriptions of the future, not observed history.
  • Forces: new influences are layered upon earlier ones; “declining” capacities do not mean earlier beings disappear.

Source method & limits

This is a structured guide to Steiner’s spiritual-scientific account, not an empirically established history of human origins. Each stage links to the source texts used for the concordance; stage assignments are more certain in some passages than others. The Ahrimanic influence belongs broadly to the middle Atlantean epoch; it should not be assigned exclusively to A4. Do not treat the Atlantean Oracles as matched one-to-one with the seven named stages.
