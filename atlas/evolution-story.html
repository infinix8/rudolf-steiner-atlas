Reading note · Source and chronology boundariesSteiner’s narrative is presented as an attributed spiritual-scientific teaching, not established prehistoric history. The seven Lemurian entries are editorial developmental milestones rather than seven formally named sequential subdivisions; some overlap. Atlantean stage names preserve historical esoteric terminology and do not describe modern ethnic groups. Post-Atlantean sixth and seventh stages are future teachings. The positions and widths of streams indicate sequence and thematic coverage only—not measurable intensity, precise dates, or the disappearance of spiritual beings. The Atlantean Oracles operate across stages, not as a one-to-one counterpart to the seven peoples.
